# Tuần 19 — Daily standup và Agile

> Giai đoạn 3 · Tuần 2/9 · Checklist gốc: [phase-3-it-english.md](../../phase-3-it-english.md#tuần-19--daily-standup-và-agile)

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Báo cáo standup trong **60–90 giây**, rõ ràng, theo khung *Yesterday / Today / Blockers*.
- Hiểu và dùng tự nhiên 35 từ Agile/Scrum: *sprint, backlog, story points, retro, estimate, velocity...*
- Ghi âm standup giả lập **5 ngày liên tiếp** (bắt đầu T4, kéo sang tuần sau nếu cần).
- Tham gia sprint planning: ước lượng, hỏi rõ yêu cầu, nói "không kịp" một cách lịch sự.
- Giải thích được **Scrum vs Kanban** trong 2 phút.

**Ngữ pháp cần cho standup** (chỉ 3 thì):
- Quá khứ đơn cho hôm qua: *I **fixed**, I **finished**, I **reviewed**.* (nhớ âm cuối -ed)
- Tương lai cho hôm nay: *I**'ll** work on..., I**'m going to**..., I **plan to**...*
- Hiện tại tiếp diễn / hoàn thành cho việc đang dở: *I**'m still working** on..., I **haven't finished** ... yet.*

## 3. Mẫu câu công việc

**Standup: hôm qua**
1. Yesterday I finished the login API and opened a PR.
2. Yesterday I worked on ticket 342, the export bug.
3. I spent most of yesterday investigating a performance issue.
4. I reviewed two PRs from Linh.
5. I paired with Tom on the payment integration.

**Standup: hôm nay**
6. Today I'll continue with the export feature.
7. Today I'm going to write unit tests for the order service.
8. I plan to finish the PR by the end of the day.
9. After that, I'll pick up the next ticket from the board.
10. I'll be in meetings most of the afternoon, so progress will be slower.

**Standup: blockers**
11. No blockers for now.
12. I'm blocked by the API team. I'm waiting for the new endpoint.
13. I need access to the staging database. Could someone help with that?
14. I have a question about the requirements. Can we talk after the standup?
15. I'm not blocked, but I might need help with Kafka later.

**Sprint planning và ước lượng**
16. How many story points would you give this?
17. I'd estimate it at five points, mostly because of the testing.
18. It's bigger than it looks. I think it's an eight.
19. Can we split this story into two smaller ones?
20. What are the acceptance criteria for this?
21. I'm not sure we can fit this into the sprint.
22. I'm off on Friday, so I have less capacity this sprint.
23. Should we do a spike first? There are too many unknowns.

**Báo trễ / thay đổi kế hoạch**
24. This is taking longer than expected.
25. I found an edge case, so I need one more day.
26. I don't think I'll finish this in this sprint. It will probably carry over.
27. Let's take this offline. (= nói riêng sau buổi họp)

**Retro**
28. What went well: we shipped on time.
29. What didn't go well: the PRs were too big and hard to review.
30. One thing we could improve is ...
31. As an action item, I suggest ...
32. I'd like to give a shout-out to Mai for helping me with the deployment.

## 4. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1: Daily standup (ba người)

> **Scrum Master (Sarah):** Morning everyone. Let's start. Nhat, do you want to go first?
> **Nhat:** Sure. Yesterday I finished the password reset API and opened a PR. I also fixed a small bug in the email template. Today I'll address the review comments and then start on the rate-limiting story. One blocker: I still don't have access to the staging Redis. Could someone help me with that?
> **Sarah:** I'll ping the DevOps team right after this. Linh?
> **Linh:** Yesterday I worked on the dashboard charts. They're about eighty percent done. Today I'll finish the filters. No blockers.
> **Sarah:** Great. Tom?
> **Tom:** I was mostly in meetings yesterday, so not much progress on the export bug. Today I'll pair with Nhat on it, if that's OK.
> **Nhat:** Sure, let's do it after lunch.
> **Sarah:** Perfect. Anything else? ... No? Thanks everyone, have a good day.

### Hội thoại 2: Sprint planning (ước lượng và phản đối nhẹ)

> **PO (David):** Next story: "As a customer, I want to export my orders to CSV." How big is it?
> **Nhat:** Before we estimate, can I ask about the acceptance criteria? Do we need all orders, or only the last twelve months?
> **David:** Good question. Let's say the last twelve months.
> **Nhat:** And how many orders can a customer have, roughly? If it's thousands, we might need to generate the file in the background and email a link.
> **David:** Most customers have fewer than a hundred, but some business accounts have more than fifty thousand.
> **Nhat:** Then I'd estimate it at eight points. The async export and the email part take time.
> **Linh:** I agree. Maybe we can split it: a simple synchronous export first, and the background job as a second story.
> **David:** I like that. Let's do the simple one this sprint.
> **Sarah:** OK, so three points for the simple export. Do we still have capacity?
> **Nhat:** I'm off on Friday, but I think we can fit it in.

## 5. Bài giải thích kỹ thuật mẫu

### T6 — Scrum vs Kanban

> Scrum and Kanban are both Agile frameworks, but they organize work in different ways.
>
> Scrum is based on fixed-length iterations called sprints, usually two weeks. At the start of each sprint, the team does sprint planning and commits to a set of items from the product backlog. During the sprint there is a short daily standup, and at the end there is a sprint review, where we demo the work, and a retrospective, where we discuss how to improve. Scrum has clear roles: a Product Owner, a Scrum Master and the developers. Changing the scope in the middle of a sprint is generally avoided.
>
> Kanban is more flexible. There are no sprints. Work flows continuously across a board with columns like "To do", "In progress", "In review" and "Done". The key rule is the WIP limit: each column can only hold a certain number of items, so the team finishes work before starting new work. Instead of velocity, Kanban teams usually measure lead time and cycle time. Priorities can change at any time.
>
> So which one is better? It depends on the type of work. Scrum fits product teams that build features and benefit from a regular rhythm. Kanban fits teams with unpredictable work, like support, operations or bug fixing. In practice, many teams use a mix of both, sometimes called "Scrumban".

**5 cụm từ dùng lại:**
1. *X and Y are both ..., but they ... in different ways.*
2. *X is based on ...*
3. *The key rule is ...*
4. *Instead of A, B teams usually ...*
5. *It depends on ... In practice, many teams ...*

**Async standup trên Slack** (nhiều team remote dùng thay họp)

```
*Yesterday*
- Finished the password reset API (PR #482)
- Reviewed Linh's PR on dashboard filters

*Today*
- Address review comments on #482
- Start the rate-limiting story (PAY-219)

*Blockers*
- Need access to staging Redis (asked DevOps)
```

**User story chuẩn**

```
As a <type of user>, I want <goal> so that <reason>.

As a customer, I want to export my orders to CSV
so that I can share them with my accountant.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I am logged in, when I click "Export", then a CSV file is downloaded.
- The file contains orders from the last 12 months.
- Columns: order ID, date, total, status.
```

**Ghi chú retro**

```
Went well:   Released the checkout redesign on time.
To improve:  PRs were too large (avg. 900 lines) and took 2+ days to review.
Action item: Keep PRs under 400 lines. Owner: everyone. Review at next retro.
```

## 7. Bài tập

1. **Ghép từ với nghĩa:** (a) spike (b) carry over (c) velocity (d) WIP limit (e) acceptance criteria — với: (1) số việc tối đa trong một cột (2) điều kiện để story được coi là đạt (3) nghiên cứu có giới hạn thời gian (4) chuyển sang sprint sau (5) số điểm trung bình team làm được mỗi sprint.
2. **Chia thì:** (a) Yesterday I ___ (finish) the API. (b) Today I ___ (work) on the tests. (c) I ___ (not / finish) the migration yet. (d) I ___ (still / wait) for the design.
3. **Sửa câu standup sai:** (a) "Yesterday I fix bug login." (b) "Today I will finished the PR." (c) "I am block by API team." (d) "I don't have blocker."
4. **Viết lại lịch sự:** "Your estimate is wrong, this is too big." → một câu phản đối nhẹ.
5. **Viết user story** cho tính năng: người dùng nhận thông báo email khi đơn hàng được giao. Kèm 3 acceptance criteria.
6. **Ghi âm:** standup thật của bạn 5 ngày liên tiếp (dùng bảng mẫu mục 5). Mỗi bản ≤ 90 giây.
7. **Nói:** giải thích Scrum vs Kanban trong 2 phút, dùng ít nhất 3 cụm từ ở mục 5.

(a) "Yesterday I fixed the login bug."
(b) "Today I will finish the PR." (sau *will* dùng động từ nguyên mẫu)

**Bài 4 (gợi ý):** "I think it might be bigger than that. Could we go through the edge cases before we agree on a number?"

**Bài 5 (gợi ý):**
```
As a customer, I want to receive an email when my order is delivered
so that I know my package has arrived.
Acceptance criteria:
- When the order status changes to DELIVERED, an email is sent within 5 minutes.
- The email contains the order ID and the delivery time.
- Users who unsubscribed from notifications do not receive the email.
```
