# Tuần 35 — Câu hỏi ngược, lương, và khi không biết câu trả lời

> Checklist gốc: [phase-4-interview.md](../../phase-4-interview.md#tuần-35--câu-hỏi-ngược-và-đàm-phán) · Cụm từ đầy đủ: [interview/phrasebook.md](../../interview/phrasebook.md)

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần này

- Chuẩn bị **5–8 câu hỏi cho interviewer** (team, quy trình, đánh giá hiệu suất, kỹ thuật).
- Nói về **lương mong muốn**, **thời gian nhận việc (notice period)**, remote/visa một cách tự tin, lịch sự.
- Xử lý khi **không biết câu trả lời** mà vẫn ghi điểm.
- **Phỏng vấn thử #7** (vòng HR đầy đủ).

## 2. Phần A — Câu hỏi dành cho interviewer

> Cuối mỗi vòng, interviewer hỏi: *"Do you have any questions for me?"* Trả lời **"No, I don't have any questions"** = mất điểm (thể hiện thiếu quan tâm). Luôn chuẩn bị **ít nhất 3 câu**, chọn câu phù hợp **người phỏng vấn** (HR, engineer, manager).

### Khung chọn câu hỏi

| Người phỏng vấn | Hỏi về | Tránh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter / HR | Quy trình tuyển dụng, văn hoá, phúc lợi, timeline | Câu hỏi kỹ thuật sâu |
| Engineer | Công việc hằng ngày, tech stack, code review, on-call, tech debt | Lương, nghỉ phép |
| Engineering manager | Kỳ vọng, đánh giá hiệu suất, lộ trình phát triển, thách thức của team | — |
| Vòng cuối / Director | Chiến lược, hướng sản phẩm, điều gì làm người thành công ở đây | — |

### 15 câu hỏi mẫu (chọn 5 để dùng)

**Về team và công việc**
1. "Could you tell me a bit about the team I'd be joining — how many people, and how the work is split?"
2. "What does a typical day or week look like for an engineer on this team?"
3. "What are the biggest technical challenges the team is facing right now?"
4. "What would you expect me to achieve in the first 3 to 6 months?"

**Về quy trình kỹ thuật**
5. "How does the team handle code reviews and deployments? How often do you release to production?"
6. "How do you balance new features with technical debt?"
7. "Is there an on-call rotation? How does it work, and how often are people paged?"
8. "How are technical decisions made — for example, is there an RFC or design review process?"

**Về phát triển và đánh giá**
9. "How is performance evaluated, and how often?"
10. "What does the career path look like for a senior engineer here — is there a staff or principal track?"
11. "What do the most successful engineers on your team have in common?"
12. "Are there opportunities for learning — like conferences, training budgets, or internal tech talks?"

**Về văn hoá và kết thúc**
13. "What do you personally enjoy most about working here?"
14. "How does the team collaborate across time zones?"
15. "What are the next steps in the process, and when can I expect to hear back?"

**Câu hỏi "chốt" mạnh (dùng cẩn thận, ở vòng cuối):**
> "Is there anything in my background or in our conversation today that makes you hesitate about my fit for this role? I'd be happy to clarify."

## 3. Phần B — Lương và thời gian nhận việc

### 3.1. Nguyên tắc

1. **Nghiên cứu trước:** dùng levels.fyi, Glassdoor, cộng đồng (ITviec, TopDev salary reports) để biết khoảng lương cho role/level/địa điểm.
2. **Đưa ra một khoảng (range)**, không phải một con số. Đầu dưới của khoảng nên là mức bạn **thực sự hài lòng**.
3. Nói lương **gross hay net**, **tháng hay năm**, **USD hay VND** cho rõ. Công ty quốc tế thường nói **annual gross** (năm, trước thuế).
4. Nếu được, để công ty đưa con số trước — nhưng **đừng né quá mức**, sẽ gây khó chịu.
5. Nói **total compensation** (tổng thu nhập): base + bonus + equity/stock + benefits.
6. Không nói dối lương hiện tại. Ở nhiều nơi bạn có quyền **không tiết lộ**.

### 3.2. Model answers

> **"What are your salary expectations?"** (vòng đầu, chưa biết nhiều)
> "Before giving a specific number, I'd love to learn more about the role and the responsibilities. Could you share the budgeted range for this position?"

> **Nếu họ vẫn yêu cầu bạn nói trước:**
> "Sure. Based on my research for senior backend roles with similar responsibilities, and on my experience, I'm looking for something in the range of **X to Y** US dollars per year, gross. That said, I'm flexible depending on the overall package — bonus, equity, and benefits like remote work and learning budget."

> **"What is your current salary?"**
> "I'd prefer to focus on the value I can bring to this role rather than my current salary, since the responsibilities are quite different. My expectation is in the range of X to Y."
> *(Nếu bạn muốn chia sẻ:)* "Currently my total compensation is around X per year, including a 13th-month salary and bonus. I'm looking for a meaningful step up for this move."

> **Khi nhận offer thấp hơn mong đợi:**
> "Thank you so much for the offer — I'm really excited about the team and the role. I was hoping for something closer to **Y**, based on the scope of the role and market data for senior engineers. Is there any flexibility on the base salary? If not, could we look at other parts of the package, like a signing bonus or extra equity?"

> **Xin thêm thời gian suy nghĩ:**
> "Thank you, I really appreciate it. Could I have until Friday to review the details and get back to you?"

### 3.3. Notice period / start date

> **"When can you start?"**
> "My contract requires a **30-day notice period**, so I could start about a month after signing. I'd like to make a proper handover to my team. If needed, I can check whether my current company is flexible about an earlier date."

> **Remote / relocation / visa:**
> "I'm currently based in Ho Chi Minh City. I'm open to relocating for the right opportunity — could you tell me whether the company supports visa sponsorship and relocation?"
> "I'm comfortable working remotely and overlapping with European hours — for example, I can be available until 9 p.m. Vietnam time."

## 4. Phần C — Khi không biết câu trả lời

**Nguyên tắc: Thành thật → Liên hệ điều bạn biết → Suy luận thành lời → Thể hiện cách sẽ tìm hiểu.**

Không bao giờ: im lặng lâu, đoán bừa như chắc chắn, hoặc bịa kinh nghiệm.

### Model answers

> **Q: "Have you worked with Kubernetes operators?"**
> "I haven't written an operator myself. I've used Kubernetes as a developer — writing deployments, configuring health probes and resource limits, and debugging pods. From what I understand, an operator is a custom controller that watches a custom resource and reconciles the actual state to the desired state — like how a Deployment controller manages ReplicaSets. If I needed to build one, I'd start with the Operator SDK or Java Operator SDK and look at how existing operators in our stack work."

> **Q: "How does the G1 garbage collector decide which regions to collect?"**
> "I don't remember the exact details of that algorithm, to be honest. What I do know is that G1 divides the heap into many equal-sized regions, and it prefers to collect the regions with the most garbage first — that's where the name 'Garbage First' comes from — to meet a pause-time goal. In practice, when we had GC problems, I looked at GC logs and tuned the heap size and `MaxGCPauseMillis`. I'd be happy to read up on the internals."

> **Q: (câu hỏi bạn hoàn toàn không biết) "What is CRDT?"**
> "I'm not familiar with that term. Could you give me a bit of context? … Ah, so it's a data structure for merging concurrent updates without coordination, used in collaborative editing. I haven't worked with it, but it sounds related to the conflict-resolution problems in eventual consistency — like last-write-wins in Cassandra. I'd be interested to learn more about how you use it."

### Mẫu câu cho tình huống này

- "I haven't worked with that directly, but I've used something similar: ..."
- "I'm not 100% sure, but my understanding is that ..."
- "I don't know the exact answer, but let me reason through it."
- "I'd have to check the documentation for the details, but I'd start by ..."
- "That's not something I've come across yet. Could you tell me a bit more?"
- "If I think about it from first principles, ..."
- "My guess would be ..., but I'd want to verify that."

## 6. Lỗi người Việt hay mắc

1. **"No, I don't have any questions"** hoặc chỉ hỏi về lương/OT ở vòng engineer.
2. **Hỏi những điều có sẵn trên website** ("What does your company do?").
3. **Ngại nói lương** → nói con số quá thấp, hoặc "Anything is okay." → Hãy nói range dựa trên dữ liệu.
4. **Không rõ đơn vị:** "I want 3,000" — 3,000 gì? USD/tháng net? Nói rõ "3,000 US dollars per month, gross".
5. **Nói dối về kinh nghiệm** khi không biết → bị hỏi sâu và lộ. Thành thật luôn tốt hơn.
6. **Im lặng khi không biết** hoặc nói "I don't know" rồi dừng. Luôn thêm "...but here's how I'd approach it".
7. **Từ vựng:** "salary hope" → "salary **expectations**"; "I must notice 30 days" → "I need to **give 30 days' notice**"; "offer letter" (✔), "I will consider" → "I'd like some time **to consider the offer**".

## 8. Prompt cho AI voice — Mock interview #7 (HR round)

```
You are a recruiter at an international tech company (pretend it's a
Singapore-based fintech with an engineering office in Vietnam). Run a full
30-minute HR / recruiter screening call for a Senior Backend Engineer role
with me. My English is B1.

Cover, in a natural order:
1. Small talk and "Tell me about yourself".
2. Why are you looking for a new job? Why us?
3. One behavioral question.
4. One technical question I probably can't answer fully (e.g. about a tool I
   haven't mentioned), to see how I handle not knowing.
5. Salary expectations - push back once: say my number is above your budget.
6. Notice period and start date; remote/relocation preferences.
7. "Do you have any questions for me?" - answer my questions realistically.
8. Close the call with next steps.

Feedback at the end:
- Did I handle the salary question confidently and clearly (currency, gross/net, range)?
- Were my questions to you good? Which one was best?
- How did I handle the question I didn't know?
- 5 language corrections.
```

## 9. Checklist tự đánh giá

- [ ] Có ít nhất 3 câu hỏi chuẩn bị sẵn, phù hợp với người phỏng vấn
- [ ] Phản hồi câu trả lời của interviewer ("That makes sense", follow-up)
- [ ] Nói lương bằng **range**, có đơn vị tiền, gross/net, tháng/năm
- [ ] Giải thích range dựa trên dữ liệu/kinh nghiệm
- [ ] Giữ thái độ tích cực khi bị trả giá
- [ ] Nói rõ notice period và ngày bắt đầu
- [ ] Khi không biết: thành thật + liên hệ điều biết + suy luận
- [ ] Kết thúc bằng lời cảm ơn và hỏi bước tiếp theo
