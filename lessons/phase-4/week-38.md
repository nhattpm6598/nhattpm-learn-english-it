## 3. Phỏng vấn thật — trước buổi phỏng vấn

### 3.1. Chuẩn bị 48 giờ trước

- [ ] Đọc lại job description, gạch 5 từ khoá. Chuẩn bị 1 câu chuyện/ví dụ cho mỗi từ khoá.
- [ ] Tìm hiểu công ty: sản phẩm, engineering blog, tin tức gần đây, interviewer trên LinkedIn.
- [ ] Viết 3 lý do "Why this company?" cụ thể cho công ty này.
- [ ] Chọn 3 câu hỏi cho interviewer phù hợp vòng này.
- [ ] Ôn intro + 3 câu chuyện STAR mạnh nhất + 2 dự án.
- [ ] Kiểm tra múi giờ! ("10 a.m. CET" = 4 p.m. giờ Việt Nam mùa đông, 3 p.m. mùa hè).

### 3.2. Chuẩn bị 1 giờ trước (online)

- [ ] Kiểm tra mic, camera, internet, sạc laptop. Mở link họp trước 5 phút.
- [ ] Phòng yên tĩnh, ánh sáng phía trước mặt, nền gọn.
- [ ] Tắt thông báo điện thoại và máy tính.
- [ ] Để sẵn: 1 cốc nước, giấy bút, CV, file ghi chú từ khoá (không đọc từ đó).
- [ ] Với coding round: mở sẵn IDE/editor quen thuộc, kiểm tra chia sẻ màn hình.
- [ ] **Khởi động giọng 10 phút:** đọc to intro 2 lần, shadow 3 phút một video tiếng Anh. Đừng vào phỏng vấn với "giọng nguội".

### 3.3. Xử lý căng thẳng

- Hít thở 4-4-4 (hít 4 giây, giữ 4, thở 4) trong 1 phút trước khi vào.
- Nhớ: interviewer **muốn bạn thành công** — họ cần tuyển người.
- Bạn không cần tiếng Anh hoàn hảo. Bạn cần **rõ ràng** và **giao tiếp được**.
- Câu "cứu cánh" nếu đầu óc trống rỗng: *"Sorry, my mind went blank for a second. Let me start again."* — hoàn toàn bình thường.

## 4. Trong buổi phỏng vấn — tình huống khó và cách xử lý

| Tình huống | Câu nói |
| --- | --- |
| Mạng lag / mất tiếng | "Sorry, I think the connection dropped. Could you repeat the question from '...'?" |
| Câu hỏi quá dài, quên ý đầu | "That's a multi-part question — let me make sure I cover everything. You asked about A, B, and C, right?" |
| Bạn trả lời sai và nhận ra | "Actually, let me correct what I said earlier — ..." |
| Interviewer có vẻ không đồng ý | "I see your point. Could you tell me what you're thinking? I might be missing something." |
| Bị hỏi dồn liên tục | Bình tĩnh. "Good question. Let me think about that for a moment." |
| Hết thời gian giữa chừng | "I realize we're running short on time. Would you like me to summarize the rest?" |
| Interviewer im lặng sau câu trả lời | "Would you like me to go into more detail on any part?" |
| Câu hỏi bất hợp pháp/riêng tư (tuổi, kết hôn) | "I'd prefer to focus on my experience for this role, if that's okay." |

## 5. Sau buổi phỏng vấn — email mẫu

### 5.1. Email cảm ơn (gửi trong 24 giờ, ngắn gọn)

```
Subject: Thank you — Senior Backend Engineer interview

Hi Anna,

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I really enjoyed
learning about the payments team, especially the work on reconciliation
and moving to an event-driven architecture.

Our conversation made me even more excited about the role. I believe my
experience with high-volume payment systems and Kafka-based services would
help the team with the challenges you described.

Please let me know if you need any additional information from me.
I look forward to hearing about the next steps.

Best regards,
Nhat Nguyen
```

### 5.2. Email hỏi tiến độ (sau thời hạn hẹn 2–3 ngày)

```
Subject: Following up — Senior Backend Engineer application

Hi David,

I hope you're doing well. I'm following up on my interview on October 10
for the Senior Backend Engineer position. I'm still very interested in the
role and wanted to check if there are any updates on the next steps.

Thank you, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Nhat Nguyen
```

### 5.3. Trả lời khi bị từ chối (giữ quan hệ tốt)

```
Subject: Re: Your application

Hi David,

Thank you for letting me know, and for the opportunity to interview with
the team. Of course I'm disappointed, but I really enjoyed the process.

If possible, I'd really appreciate any feedback that could help me improve.
I'd also be happy to be considered for future roles that match my profile.

Best regards,
Nhat Nguyen
```

### 5.4. Chấp nhận offer

```
Subject: Offer acceptance — Senior Backend Engineer

Hi David,

Thank you very much for the offer. I'm delighted to accept the position of
Senior Backend Engineer, with a start date of December 1, as discussed.

Please let me know what the next steps are and if you need any documents
from me. I'm really looking forward to joining the team.

Best regards,
Nhat Nguyen
```

### 5.5. Xin dời lịch phỏng vấn

```

Unfortunately, something urgent has come up and I won't be able to attend
our interview on October 15 at 3 p.m. I'm very sorry for the inconvenience.

time between 2 p.m. and 6 p.m. (GMT+7).

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
Nhat Nguyen
```

## 7. Model answer — vòng "hiring manager" thật (câu hay gặp)

> **"Why should we hire you?"**
> "I think I bring three things that match what you need. First, I've spent the last three years building exactly the kind of system you're working on — high-volume payment services with Spring Boot and Kafka — so I can contribute quickly. Second, I care about reliability: I've led post-mortems, improved monitoring, and I'm comfortable being on call. And third, I can work across the stack and with non-engineers, which matters for a small team that owns features end-to-end. I'm also very motivated to grow in an international team, and I've invested a lot this year in my English to be ready for that."

> **"Do you have other offers / are you interviewing elsewhere?"**
> "Yes, I'm in the process with two other companies, one at the final stage. But to be honest, this role is my first choice because of [specific reason]. If there's a way to align timelines, that would be great."

> **"How do you handle working with people in different time zones?"**
> "In my previous company, I worked with clients in Australia and Japan, so I'm used to it. I rely a lot on clear written communication — I write a short summary after discussions, and I document decisions in tickets so people can catch up asynchronously. I also try to protect the overlapping hours for meetings and use the rest for focused work."

> **"What kind of manager do you work best with?"**
> "I work best with a manager who gives clear goals and context — the 'why' — and then trusts me to figure out the 'how'. I appreciate regular one-on-ones and direct feedback, both positive and negative. In return, I keep my manager updated proactively, especially about risks."

## 9. Lỗi người Việt hay mắc

1. **Không gửi email cảm ơn** — ở nhiều công ty quốc tế, email cảm ơn ngắn là phép lịch sự được đánh giá cao.
2. **Email quá dài, quá trang trọng** ("Dear Sir/Madam, I am writing this letter to express my deepest gratitude..."). Viết ngắn, thân thiện.
3. **Sai múi giờ** → vào muộn 1 tiếng. Luôn xác nhận: "Just to confirm, that's 3 p.m. Vietnam time (GMT+7), right?"
4. **Không ghi lại câu hỏi** sau phỏng vấn thật → mất dữ liệu quý nhất để luyện tiếp.
5. **Nản sau lần bị từ chối đầu tiên.** Tỉ lệ bình thường: cần nhiều buổi phỏng vấn mới có 1 offer. Mỗi buổi là 1 buổi luyện miễn phí.
6. **"Why should we hire you?" → trả lời khiêm tốn** "I think I'm not the best, but I will try my best." → Hãy tự tin nêu 3 lý do.

## 11. Prompt cho AI voice — Mock interview #10 (final)

```
This is my 10th and final mock interview. Make it as realistic and as
challenging as a real final-round interview at a good international tech company.

Role: Senior Backend Engineer (Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, PostgreSQL, AWS).
You are the hiring manager. Duration: 45-50 minutes. Speak at natural speed.

Include:
- Short small talk and intro.
- "Tell me about yourself" and "Why should we hire you?"
- 2 behavioral questions with tough follow-ups.
- A 15-minute system design or production-debugging discussion.
- A question I probably don't know the answer to.
- A salary expectations question.
- My questions for you.

At the end, give a detailed evaluation:
1. Hire / no hire at Senior level, and your reasoning.
2. Compare me to a typical candidate with native-level English: what's the
   real gap, and is it a blocker?
3. The 3 most important things to fix before my next real interview.
4. 10 language corrections.
```
