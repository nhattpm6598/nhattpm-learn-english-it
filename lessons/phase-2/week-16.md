# Tuần 16 — Giải thích, hướng dẫn

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-16--giải-thích-hướng-dẫn). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Giải thích một quy trình theo từng bước: nấu một món ăn, cài một ứng dụng, cách một app hoạt động.
- Dùng cụm "làm rõ ý": *Basically..., What I mean is..., In other words..., Let me put it another way.*
- Giải thích khái niệm IT cho người không biết IT bằng **so sánh đời thường** (API giống người phục vụ nhà hàng...).
- Dùng **câu bị động hiện tại đơn** để mô tả hệ thống: *The data is stored in a database. The request is sent to the server.*
- Kiểm tra người nghe hiểu chưa: *Does that make sense?*

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Hướng dẫn nấu phở bò (giản lược)

```
Tutor: So you're going to teach me how to make a Vietnamese dish?
Nhat:  Yes, I'll explain how to make beef pho. Well, a simple home version.
Tutor: Great! What do I need?
Nhat:  Basically, you need beef bones, beef slices, rice noodles, onions,
       ginger and some spices like star anise and cinnamon.
Tutor: Okay. What's the first step?
Nhat:  First, you grill the onions and the ginger until they're a bit black.
       It gives the soup a smoky flavor.
Tutor: Interesting. Then?
Nhat:  Then you boil the bones in water for about five minutes and throw the
       water away. This makes the soup clear.
Tutor: Ah, so you clean the bones first.
Nhat:  Exactly. After that, you put the bones in fresh water with the onions,
       ginger and spices, and you simmer it for at least six hours.
Tutor: Six hours! That's a long time.
Nhat:  Yes, that's the secret. Make sure you don't let it boil too hard,
       or the soup becomes cloudy.
Tutor: And at the end?
Nhat:  At the end, you put the noodles and raw beef slices in a bowl and pour
       the hot soup on top. The hot soup cooks the beef.
Tutor: So the beef is cooked in the bowl?
Nhat:  Yes, in other words, it's cooked by the soup. Then you add herbs,
       lime and chili. And that's it!
Tutor: Sounds delicious. Does that make your kitchen smell good for hours?
Nhat:  For days, actually!
```

Ghi chú: *grill* — nướng; *simmer* — ninh lửa nhỏ; *cloudy* — đục; *pour* — rót, chan; *star anise* — hoa hồi; *cinnamon* — quế.

### Hội thoại 2 — Giải thích app Grab hoạt động thế nào

```
Tutor: You use Grab every day, right? How does it actually work?
Nhat:  Okay, in simple terms, there are three parts: the customer app, the
       driver app and the server in the middle.
Tutor: The server is like the brain?
Nhat:  Exactly. When you book a ride, your location is sent to the server.
Tutor: Then what?
Nhat:  The server looks for drivers near you. It checks their location, which
       is updated every few seconds.
Tutor: How does it choose one driver?
Nhat:  It's a bit like a matching game. The system looks at distance, traffic
       and the driver's rating, and then it sends your request to the best
       driver.
Tutor: What if the driver says no?
Nhat:  Then the request is sent to the next driver. That's why sometimes it
       takes a minute to find someone.
Tutor: And how is the price calculated?
Nhat:  The price is calculated before you book. It depends on the distance,
       the time and how many people want a ride at that moment.
Tutor: Ah, so it's more expensive when it rains?
Nhat:  Right. More people want a ride, but there are fewer drivers. Does that
       make sense?
Tutor: Perfectly. You explained it really well.
```

Lưu ý câu bị động: *your location **is sent**, it **is updated**, the price **is calculated**.*

### Hội thoại 3 — Giải thích "API" cho người không biết IT

```
Mom's friend: So what's an API? My son always talks about it.
Nhat: Let me put it this way. Imagine you're at a restaurant.
Mom's friend: Okay.
Nhat: You're the customer, and the kitchen makes the food. But you don't go
      into the kitchen yourself, right?
Mom's friend: No, the waiter takes my order.
Nhat: Exactly. The API is like the waiter. One app gives its order to the API,
      the API takes it to another system, and then it brings back the result.
Mom's friend: So, for example?
Nhat: For example, when you check the weather on your phone, the weather app
      doesn't measure the weather itself. It asks a weather service through
      an API.
Mom's friend: Oh, so the API carries messages between programs?
Nhat: Yes, in other words, it's how different programs talk to each other.
Mom's friend: That's much easier than I thought.
Nhat: The idea is simple. The hard part is building it well!
```

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Câu bị động hiện tại đơn (mô tả hệ thống, quy trình)

Khi giải thích hệ thống, cái quan trọng là **việc gì xảy ra với dữ liệu**, không phải **ai làm** → dùng bị động.

**Cấu trúc:** am / is / are + **V3 (past participle)**

| Chủ động | Bị động |
| --- | --- |
| The server stores your password. | Your password **is stored** on the server. |
| The app sends a notification. | A notification **is sent** to your phone. |
| We deploy the code every Friday. | The code **is deployed** every Friday. |
| The system checks all orders. | All orders **are checked** by the system. |

- Thêm **by + người/vật** chỉ khi cần nói ai làm: *The report is generated **by** a cron job.*
- Câu hỏi: **Is** the data **encrypted**? / How **is** the price **calculated**?
- Phủ định: Passwords **are not stored** in plain text.

V3 hay dùng: send → sent, store → stored, save → saved, build → built, write → written, show → shown, make → made, keep → kept, use → used, test → tested.

### Bài tập

Chuyển sang bị động hoặc điền dạng đúng.

1. The app saves your photos in the cloud. → Your photos ___
2. Every request ___ (check) by the API gateway.
3. The emails ___ (send) at 8 a.m. every day.
4. How ___ the price ___ (calculate)?
5. Sửa lỗi: *Can you explain me how the data is store?*

8. What is an API? Use a real-life comparison.

10. What is a bug, and how do developers find it?
11. Explain how your team releases a new feature, from idea to production.
12. What is a password manager, and why should people use one?
13. Explain the rules of a game or sport you like.
14. What's the best way to learn English, in your opinion? Explain your method.

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q7. What is "the cloud"? Explain it to your parents.**

> Basically, "the cloud" just means other people's computers. Big companies like Google and Amazon have huge buildings full of computers, called data centers. When you save a photo "in the cloud", your photo is sent through the internet and stored on one of those computers, not on your phone. In other words, it's like putting your things in a bank instead of keeping them at home. If you lose your phone, your photos are still safe, and you can see them from any device. Does that make sense?

**Q9. What happens when you type a website address into a browser?**

> In simple terms, there are four main steps. First, the browser needs to find the address of the server, a bit like looking up a phone number in a phone book. This is done by something called DNS. Once that's done, the browser sends a request to the server. Then the server processes the request and sends back the page, usually as HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Finally, the browser reads these files and shows the page on your screen. All of this happens in less than a second.

**Q11. How does your team release a new feature?**

> First, the product owner writes the requirements in a ticket. Then the ticket is discussed in sprint planning. After that, a developer builds the feature on a separate branch and opens a pull request. The code is reviewed by at least one teammate, and the tests are run automatically. Once it's approved, it's merged and deployed to a staging environment, where QA tests it. If everything is fine, it's released to production, usually behind a feature flag.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
I want to practice explaining things clearly in English. I'm a Vietnamese
software engineer at about A2-B1 level.

Please act as a curious person who knows NOTHING about technology, like my
grandmother or a 12-year-old kid.

1. Ask me to explain one thing, for example: "What is the cloud?",
   "How does Grab find a driver?", "What is an API?", "What do you do at work?"
2. While I explain, interrupt with simple questions like "Wait, what's a
   server?" or "Can you give me an example?" If I use technical words,
   ask what they mean.
3. When I finish, tell me: was it clear? Which part was confusing?
4. Correct my grammar briefly, especially passive voice (is stored, is sent)
   and "explain me".
5. Then show me a clearer version of my explanation in about 100 words.
Let's do 3 topics.
```

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: hướng dẫn gia sư nấu một món Việt. Họ đóng vai người chưa nấu bao giờ, hỏi nhiều.
- Buổi 2: giải thích công việc của mình cho gia sư (người không biết IT). Nhờ gia sư nói lại bằng lời của họ — nếu họ nói lại đúng là bạn giải thích tốt.
- Hỏi gia sư: "When native speakers explain something, what words do they use instead of 'firstly, secondly'?"
