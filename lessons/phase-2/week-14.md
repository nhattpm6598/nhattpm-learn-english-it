# Tuần 14 — Kể chuyện, trải nghiệm

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-14--kể-chuyện-trải-nghiệm). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Kể một câu chuyện 2–3 phút có mở đầu, diễn biến, cao trào, kết thúc, dùng từ nối (*first, then, after that, suddenly, eventually*).
- Phân biệt **quá khứ đơn** và **quá khứ tiếp diễn**: *I was deploying when the server crashed.*
- Kể một lần gặp khó khăn trong công việc theo khung **STAR** (Situation – Task – Action – Result) — nền tảng cho phỏng vấn hành vi ở giai đoạn 4.
- Phản ứng khi nghe người khác kể: *What happened next? That must have been stressful!*

## 2. Mẫu câu chính

### 2.1. Mở đầu câu chuyện

| Câu | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Let me tell you about ... | Để tôi kể về ... | Mở đầu |
| This happened about two years ago. | Chuyện này xảy ra khoảng 2 năm trước. | Thời điểm |
| A few years ago, when I was working at ... | Vài năm trước, khi tôi làm ở ... | Bối cảnh |
| I'll never forget the time when ... | Tôi sẽ không bao giờ quên lần ... | Chuyện đáng nhớ |
| It was my first week at a new company. | Đó là tuần đầu tôi ở công ty mới. | Bối cảnh |

### 2.2. Từ nối theo thứ tự thời gian

| Cụm | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First, / At first, | Đầu tiên / Lúc đầu (sau đó thay đổi) | At first, I thought it was a small bug. |
| Then, / After that, / Next, | Sau đó | After that, we checked the logs. |
| Meanwhile, / While ... | Trong khi đó | Meanwhile, customers were complaining. |
| Suddenly, / All of a sudden, | Bỗng nhiên | Suddenly, the whole system went down. |
| As soon as ... | Ngay khi ... | As soon as I saw the error, I called my lead. |
| A few minutes later, / The next day, | Vài phút sau / Hôm sau | The next day, we found the cause. |
| Luckily, / Unfortunately, | May mắn là / Không may là | Luckily, we had a backup. |
| In the end, / Eventually, / Finally, | Cuối cùng | Eventually, we fixed it at 2 a.m. |

### 2.3. Cảm xúc và đánh giá

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| I was really nervous / stressed / relieved. | Tôi rất lo / căng thẳng / nhẹ nhõm. |
| I had no idea what to do. | Tôi không biết phải làm gì. |
| It was a nightmare. | Đó là cơn ác mộng. |
| It turned out that ... | Hóa ra là ... |
| Looking back, I think ... | Nhìn lại, tôi nghĩ ... |
| I learned that ... / It taught me to ... | Tôi học được rằng ... / Nó dạy tôi ... |
| Next time, I would ... | Lần sau tôi sẽ ... |

### 2.4. Khung STAR cho chuyện công việc

| Phần | Câu mẫu |
| --- | --- |
| **S**ituation (bối cảnh) | We were building a booking system for a hotel chain. Two weeks before launch, ... |
| **T**ask (nhiệm vụ của bạn) | My job was to ... / I was responsible for ... / I needed to ... |
| **A**ction (bạn đã làm gì — dùng **I**, không phải *we*) | First, I ... Then I ... I also suggested ... |
| **R**esult (kết quả, có số liệu nếu được) | As a result, ... / We launched on time. / Response time dropped by 60%. |

### 2.5. Khi nghe người khác kể

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| What happened next? / And then? | Rồi sao nữa? |
| Oh no! / That's terrible! / That's hilarious! | Ôi không! / Kinh khủng! / Buồn cười quá! |
| That must have been stressful. | Chắc là căng thẳng lắm. |
| How did you feel? | Bạn cảm thấy thế nào? |
| What would you do differently? | Bạn sẽ làm khác đi thế nào? |

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Kể một chuyến đi

```
Tutor: Have you traveled anywhere interesting recently?
Nhat:  Yes, last summer my family went to Phu Quoc. It's an island in the south
       of Vietnam.
Tutor: Oh, how was it?
Nhat:  Mostly great, but something funny happened on the first day.
Tutor: Tell me!
Nhat:  Well, we arrived at the hotel in the afternoon. While my wife was
       checking in, my son was playing near the pool.
Tutor: Uh-oh.
Nhat:  Exactly. Suddenly, I heard a big splash. He fell into the pool with all
       his clothes on.
Tutor: Oh no! Was he okay?
Nhat:  Yes, luckily it was the kids' pool, so it wasn't deep. A staff member
       pulled him out in two seconds.
Tutor: That must have been scary.
Nhat:  It was, for a moment. But then he started laughing, and he wanted to
       jump in again!
Tutor: Ha! Kids! What did you do for the rest of the trip?
Nhat:  We swam, ate a lot of seafood and visited a pepper farm. In the end,
       it was one of our best holidays.
Tutor: And did he learn to stay away from the pool?
Nhat:  Not really. Now he wants swimming lessons!
```

Ghi chú: *splash* — tiếng nước bắn tung tóe; *with all his clothes on* — mặc nguyên quần áo; *for the rest of the trip* — phần còn lại của chuyến đi.

### Hội thoại 2 — Sự cố production (khung STAR)

```
Interviewer: Can you tell me about a difficult problem you faced at work?
Nhat: Sure. About two years ago, I was working on an e-commerce platform.
      One Friday evening, during a big sale, the checkout page suddenly
      became very slow.
Interviewer: What was your role?
Nhat: I was the on-call engineer that night, so I needed to find the problem
      and fix it as quickly as possible.
Interviewer: What did you do?
Nhat: First, I checked the monitoring dashboard. The database CPU was at a
      hundred percent. Then I looked at the slow query log, and I found one
      query that was running thousands of times per minute.
Interviewer: What was causing it?
Nhat: It turned out that a new feature was loading the product list without
      a cache. So I turned off that feature with a feature flag, and the
      system went back to normal in about five minutes.
Interviewer: And after that?
Nhat: The next Monday, I added caching and an index, and I wrote a short
      post-mortem for the team.
Interviewer: What was the result?
Nhat: We didn't lose the sale, and after the fix, the page was about three
      times faster than before. I also learned to always load-test new
      features before a big event.
```

Ghi chú: *on-call* — trực sự cố; *it turned out that* — hóa ra; *feature flag* — cờ bật/tắt tính năng; *post-mortem* — báo cáo phân tích sau sự cố.

### Hội thoại 3 — Một ngày tệ hại

```
Amy:  You look tired. Rough day?
Nhat: Yeah, you won't believe what happened this morning.
Amy:  What?
Nhat: I was riding to work when it started raining really heavily. I didn't
      have a raincoat, so I stopped under a bridge.
Amy:  Oh no.
Nhat: While I was waiting, my phone rang. It was my manager. He reminded me
      about the client demo at nine.
Amy:  And what time was it?
Nhat: Eight forty-five! So I rode in the rain and arrived completely wet.
Amy:  Did you do the demo like that?
Nhat: Yes, but luckily it was online, so I turned off my camera!
Amy:  Ha! Smart move. How did it go?
Nhat: Actually, it went really well. The client was happy.
Amy:  So all's well that ends well.
```

Ghi chú: *Rough day?* — Hôm nay tệ à?; *Smart move* — nước đi khôn; *All's well that ends well* — kết thúc có hậu là được.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Quá khứ đơn vs Quá khứ tiếp diễn

| | Quá khứ đơn | Quá khứ tiếp diễn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cấu trúc | V-ed / V2 | was/were + V-ing |
| Dùng cho | Hành động ngắn, hoàn tất, chuỗi sự kiện | Hành động đang diễn ra (bối cảnh) thì bị chen ngang |
| Ví dụ | The server **crashed**. | I **was deploying** the new version... |

**Kết hợp với *when* và *while*:**
- I **was deploying** the new version **when** the server **crashed**. (đang làm A thì B xảy ra)
- **While** I **was waiting** for the bus, it **started** to rain.
- **While** I **was coding**, my colleague **was writing** tests. (2 việc song song)

**Chuỗi sự kiện liên tiếp** → chỉ dùng quá khứ đơn:
- I **opened** the logs, **found** the error and **fixed** it.

### Bài tập

1. I ___ (have) lunch when my manager ___ (call) me.
2. While we ___ (test) the app, we ___ (find) a serious bug.
3. What ___ you ___ (do) at 10 p.m. last night?
4. She ___ (open) the file, ___ (read) the error and ___ (restart) the service.
5. Sửa lỗi: *When I was arriving at the office, the meeting already was starting.*

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

1. Tell me about the best trip you've ever taken.
2. Tell me about a time something went wrong on a trip.
3. What's the funniest thing that has happened to you at work?
4. Tell me about your first day at your current job.
5. Describe a time you made a mistake at work. What did you learn?
6. Tell me about a difficult bug you fixed.
7. Tell me about a time you worked under a tight deadline.
8. Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate. How did you handle it?
9. Tell me about a project you're proud of.
10. Tell me about a time you learned something new very quickly.
11. What's the most embarrassing moment you've had in a meeting?
12. Describe a day when everything went wrong.
13. Tell me about a person who helped you in your career.

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q5. Describe a time you made a mistake at work.** (STAR)

> **(S)** In my second year as a developer, I was working on a payroll system for a client. **(T)** I had to update a script that calculated overtime pay. **(A)** I tested it on my local machine, but I didn't test it with real data from production. When we ran it at the end of the month, some employees got the wrong amount. As soon as I found out, I told my team lead. I didn't try to hide it. Then I fixed the calculation, and I wrote a script to find every wrong payment. **(R)** We corrected all the payments within one day, and the client was okay with it. Since then, I always ask for a copy of real data, without personal information, to test important changes.

**Q1. Tell me about the best trip you've ever taken.**

> The best trip I've ever taken was to Ha Giang, in the north of Vietnam, about five years ago. My friends and I rented motorbikes and rode through the mountains for four days. The views were amazing. On the second day, my bike broke down in the middle of nowhere. Luckily, a local family helped us. They invited us to have lunch with them while their son fixed the bike. We couldn't speak their language, but we laughed a lot. In the end, that was my favorite memory of the whole trip.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
I want to practice telling stories in English. I'm a Vietnamese software
engineer at about A2-B1 level.

Please:
1. Ask me to tell you a story about [a trip / a difficult problem at work /
   a mistake I made]. Let me speak for 1-2 minutes without interrupting.
2. Then ask me 4-5 follow-up questions about details: when, where, who,
   what happened next, how I felt, what I learned.
3. Check my past tenses. If I use present tense for past events, or mix up
   "I was doing" and "I did", correct me briefly.
4. For work stories, check if my story follows STAR (Situation, Task, Action,
   Result). Tell me which part was weak, and remind me to say "I" instead of
   "we" for my actions.
5. Finally, retell my story in better English (about 120 words) so I can
   shadow it.
```

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: kể một chuyến đi. Nhờ gia sư ngắt lời hỏi chi tiết như người nghe thật.
- Buổi 2: kể chuyện "một lần gặp khó khăn trong công việc" theo STAR. Hỏi: "Was my story clear? Which part was too long?"
- Viết lại câu chuyện STAR sau khi được sửa, lưu vào `interview/answers.md` — sẽ dùng lại ở giai đoạn 4.

## 9. Đáp án

Bài tập ngữ pháp (mục 5):

1. I **was having** lunch when my manager **called** me.
2. While we **were testing** the app, we **found** a serious bug.
3. What **were** you **doing** at 10 p.m. last night?
4. She **opened** the file, **read** the error and **restarted** the service.
5. When I **arrived** at the office, the meeting **had already started**. (*had started* là quá khứ hoàn thành — việc xảy ra trước một việc khác trong quá khứ. Cách đơn giản hơn: *When I arrived at the office, the meeting **was already in progress**.*)
