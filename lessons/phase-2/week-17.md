# Tuần 17 — Tổng ôn giai đoạn 2

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-17--tổng-ôn). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Nói **15 phút liên tục** với gia sư hoặc AI, không chuyển sang tiếng Việt (mốc kiểm tra của giai đoạn).
- Chuyển chủ đề mượt: từ giới thiệu bản thân → công việc → ý kiến → kể chuyện, như một cuộc trò chuyện thật.
- Tìm và sửa **lỗi lặp lại** của chính mình (nghe lại bản ghi âm tuần 9–16).
- Có sẵn "bộ khung" để không bị bí: câu cứu hộ, câu kéo dài thời gian, câu chuyển chủ đề.

## 2. Mẫu câu chính — bộ "giữ hội thoại" tổng hợp

### 2.1. Câu cứu hộ (tuần 9, phải phản xạ)

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| Could you say that again, please? | Bạn nói lại được không? |
| Sorry, could you speak a bit more slowly? | Bạn nói chậm hơn chút được không? |
| What do you mean by ...? | ... nghĩa là gì? |
| Let me think about that for a second. | Để tôi nghĩ chút. |
| How do you say ... in English? | ... tiếng Anh nói thế nào? |

### 2.2. Khi quên từ — mô tả thay vì dừng lại (không chuyển sang tiếng Việt)

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| I don't know the word, but it's a thing you use to ... | Tôi không biết từ đó, nhưng nó là thứ dùng để ... |
| It's a kind of ... / It's similar to ... | Nó là một loại ... / Nó giống ... |
| It's the opposite of ... | Nó ngược với ... |
| What's the word...? It's on the tip of my tongue. | Từ gì nhỉ... tôi sắp nhớ ra rồi. |
| Sorry, I mean ... | Xin lỗi, ý tôi là ... (tự sửa) |

### 2.3. Câu kéo dài thời gian suy nghĩ

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu — trò chuyện tự do nhiều chủ đề

Mục đích: thấy một cuộc nói chuyện 15 phút "chảy" qua nhiều chủ đề thế nào. Đọc to, sau đó tự đóng vai Nhat với AI.

### Hội thoại 1 — Từ chào hỏi sang công việc

```
Tutor: Hi Nhat! How's your week been so far?
Nhat:  Pretty busy, actually. We have a release on Friday, so everyone is
       working hard.
Tutor: Oh, a release. What are you releasing?
Nhat:  It's a new reporting feature for our clients. Basically, they can
       download their monthly transactions as a file.
Tutor: Sounds useful. Are you nervous about it?
Nhat:  A little. Last time we released something big, we had a problem with
       the database.
Tutor: What happened?
Nhat:  It's a long story, but in short, one query was too slow, and the page
       stopped loading. We fixed it in about an hour.
Tutor: That must have been stressful.
Nhat:  It was. But it taught us to test with real data. So this time, we
       did a load test last week.
Tutor: Good thinking. Speaking of work, do you usually work late before a
       release?
Nhat:  Sometimes, but I try not to. I think working late every day is bad for
       the team in the long run.
Tutor: I agree. So what do you do to relax after a busy week?
Nhat:  I usually go running on Saturday morning. It helps me clear my head.
```

### Hội thoại 2 — Ý kiến, sở thích, kế hoạch

```
Tutor: You've been learning English for a few months now. How do you feel?
Nhat:  Hmm, let me think. I feel more confident. At first, I translated
       everything in my head from Vietnamese. Now I can answer simple
       questions directly.
Tutor: That's great progress. What's still difficult?
Nhat:  Listening to fast speakers, especially in meetings. And I still forget
       the "s" sometimes, like "he work" instead of "he works".
Tutor: Yes, I noticed that a couple of times today.
Nhat:  Really? Could you tell me when I do it?
Tutor: Sure, I'll note them down. What do you think is the best way to
       learn English?
Nhat:  In my opinion, speaking every day is the most important. Grammar books
       are useful, but if you don't speak, you can't use the grammar.
Tutor: Some people say watching movies is the best way.
Nhat:  I see your point, but movies are too fast for my level. I prefer
       podcasts for learners, like 6 Minute English.
Tutor: Fair enough. What's your next goal?
Nhat:  I want to learn how to talk about my technical work, and in about six
       months, I want to do a real job interview in English.
Tutor: That's very doable. I'm sure you'll get there.
Nhat:  Thank you. That really motivates me.
```

Ghi chú: *in the long run* — về lâu dài; *note them down* — ghi lại; *doable* — làm được; *get there* — đạt được mục tiêu.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — 8 lỗi người Việt hay lặp lại

| # | Lỗi | Sai | Đúng |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Quên -s ngôi thứ 3 | My brother work in Hanoi. | My brother **works** in Hanoi. |
| 2 | Quên quá khứ khi kể chuyện | Yesterday I go to the office. | Yesterday I **went** to the office. |
| 3 | Dùng *be* + động từ | I'm agree. / I'm work at FPT. | I **agree**. / I **work** at FPT. |
| 4 | Thiếu mạo từ a/an/the | I'm software engineer. | I'm **a** software engineer. |
| 5 | Sai số nhiều | I have many experience. / two year | I have **a lot of** experience. / two **years** |
| 6 | Câu hỏi thiếu trợ động từ | Where you live? | Where **do** you live? |
| 7 | "explain me", "discuss about" | Can you explain me? Let's discuss about it. | Can you **explain it to me**? Let's **discuss it**. |
| 8 | Bỏ âm cuối khi nói | /wɝːk/ cho "worked" | Nói rõ /wɝːkt/, /lɪvz/, /dʒɑːbz/ |

Ghi thêm vào bảng này **lỗi riêng của bạn** sau khi nghe lại bản ghi âm (T2 của tuần).

### Bài tập — sửa lỗi

Mỗi câu có 1–2 lỗi.

1. My wife is teacher, she teach English at a high school.
2. Last weekend I don't go anywhere, I just stay home.
3. I'm not agree with you, remote work is more better.
4. Can you explain me how this API work?
5. I am working here since three year.

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói — bài kiểm tra 15 phút

Đây là "đề" cho buổi mốc kiểm tra (T5 với AI, T6 với gia sư). Người hỏi chọn ngẫu nhiên, bạn trả lời mỗi câu 1–2 phút, **không chuyển sang tiếng Việt**.

1. Tell me about yourself and your family.
2. What does a normal day look like for you?
3. What do you do in your free time? How did you get into it?
4. What did you do last weekend?
5. What do you do, and what are you working on at the moment?
6. What do you like and dislike about your job?
7. Remote work or office work — which is better? Why?
8. Should developers use AI to write code?
9. Tell me about a trip you'll never forget.
10. Tell me about a difficult problem you solved at work.
11. Imagine you need to move our next lesson. What would you say?
12. Explain how a food delivery app works to someone who doesn't know technology.
13. How has your English changed in the last two months?
14. What are your goals for the next six months?
15. If you could work anywhere in the world, where would it be and why?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q13. How has your English changed in the last two months?**

> I think my English has improved a lot, especially my speaking. Two months ago, I was very nervous when a tutor asked me a question. I always translated from Vietnamese in my head, so my answers were slow and short. Now I can talk about familiar topics like my job, my family and my hobbies without thinking too much. I also use phrases like "Let me think" or "Could you say that again?" when I need time, so I don't panic. But I still need to work on my listening, especially when people speak fast, and I sometimes forget the "s" at the end of verbs. My next step is to learn how to talk about technical topics.

**Q15. If you could work anywhere, where would it be?**

> That's a good question. I think I'd choose Singapore or somewhere in Europe, like the Netherlands. The main reason is that they have many international tech companies, so I could work with people from different countries. Singapore is also close to Vietnam, so I could visit my parents easily. On the other hand, Europe has a better work-life balance, from what I've heard. So it depends on my family's situation. For now, I'd be happy with a remote job for an international company, so I can stay in Vietnam.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI — 15 phút liên tục (T5)

```
This is my end-of-phase speaking test. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer.
I've studied conversational English for 9 weeks, and my level is about A2-B1.

Please:
1. Talk with me for 15 minutes like a friendly native speaker. Move naturally
   between these topics: myself and my family, my daily routine, hobbies and
   last weekend, my job, an opinion question about technology or work,
   a story from my life, and explaining how something works.
2. Do NOT correct me during the conversation. Just keep it going, ask
   follow-up questions, and push me to give longer answers.
3. If I use Vietnamese or stop for too long, gently say "Try to say it in
   another way" and wait.
4. After 15 minutes, give me a report:
   - Estimated CEFR level for speaking (A2, B1, B1+...) and why.
   - Fluency, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation (as far as you can tell):
     one strength and one weakness each.
   - My 5 most repeated mistakes, with the correct version.
   - 3 things to focus on in the next phase (English for IT work).
```

### Mốc kiểm tra với gia sư (T6)

Nói với gia sư đầu buổi:

> "Today is my end-of-phase test. Could we talk freely for 15 minutes about different topics? Please don't correct me during the conversation. At the end, could you tell me my level, my biggest strengths and weaknesses, and my most common mistakes?"

Câu hỏi để hỏi gia sư cuối buổi:
1. What level do you think I'm at now?
2. What is the one thing I should improve first?
3. Did I sound natural, or did I sound like I was translating?
4. Which of my mistakes make me hard to understand?
5. Do you think I'm ready to start learning English for IT meetings and interviews?

### Nhật ký giai đoạn (T7)

Viết vào `log/progress.md`:
- Kết quả 15 phút: có chuyển sang tiếng Việt không? Mấy lần bí?
- Trình độ AI và gia sư đánh giá.
- 5 lỗi lặp lại nhiều nhất (từ bảng ở mục 5).
- 3 điều làm tốt nhất trong 9 tuần.
- Quyết định: sang giai đoạn 3, hay lặp lại 1–2 tuần yếu nhất.
