# Tuần 15 — Hẹn lịch, điện thoại, họp online

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-15--hẹn-lịch-điện-thoại-họp-online). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Đặt / đổi / hủy lịch họp bằng lời nói và tin nhắn, có múi giờ.
- Xử lý các tình huống họp online: mất tiếng, share màn hình, mạng lag, ngắt lời lịch sự.
- Nghe máy và trả lời cuộc gọi đầu tiên với recruiter (screening call) một cách tự tin.
- Dùng đúng giới từ thời gian **at / on / in** và cách nói lịch hẹn tương lai (*I'm meeting... / I'll...*).

## 2. Mẫu câu chính

### 2.1. Đặt lịch

| Câu | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are you available on Tuesday afternoon? | Chiều thứ 3 bạn rảnh không? | Hỏi lịch |
| Does Tuesday at 3 p.m. work for you? | 3 giờ chiều thứ 3 được không? | Đề xuất cụ thể |
| What time works best for you? | Giờ nào tiện nhất cho bạn? | Để người kia chọn |
| How about Thursday morning instead? | Hay sáng thứ 5 thì sao? | Đề xuất khác |
| I'm free any time after 2 p.m. Vietnam time. | Tôi rảnh bất cứ lúc nào sau 2 giờ chiều giờ VN. | Kèm múi giờ |
| Which time zone are you in? | Bạn ở múi giờ nào? | Họp quốc tế |
| So that's 9 a.m. your time, 3 p.m. my time, right? | Vậy là 9h sáng giờ bạn, 3h chiều giờ tôi nhé? | Xác nhận |
| I'll send you a calendar invite. | Tôi sẽ gửi lời mời qua lịch. | Chốt lịch |
| Let's say 30 minutes. | Cứ để 30 phút nhé. | Thời lượng |

### 2.2. Đổi, hủy lịch, đến muộn

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| Something came up. Could we reschedule? | Tôi có việc đột xuất. Mình dời lịch được không? |
| Would it be possible to move our meeting to Friday? | Có thể dời buổi họp sang thứ 6 không? |
| I'm sorry, but I have to cancel today's meeting. | Xin lỗi, tôi phải hủy buổi họp hôm nay. |
| I'm running five minutes late. | Tôi đến muộn 5 phút. |
| Sorry, I'm double-booked at that time. | Xin lỗi, giờ đó tôi bị trùng lịch. |

### 2.3. Gọi điện thoại

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| Hello, this is Nhat speaking. | A lô, Nhật đây. (nghe máy công việc) |
| Hi, this is Nhat from ABC. Is this a good time to talk? | Chào, tôi là Nhật từ ABC. Giờ nói chuyện tiện không? |
| Could I speak to Ms. Johnson, please? | Cho tôi gặp chị Johnson được không? |
| Sorry, you're breaking up. | Xin lỗi, tiếng bạn bị ngắt quãng. |
| Could you spell that for me, please? | Bạn đánh vần giúp tôi được không? |
| Let me just write that down. | Để tôi ghi lại. |
| Can I call you back in ten minutes? | 10 phút nữa tôi gọi lại được không? |
| Thanks for calling. Talk to you soon. | Cảm ơn đã gọi. Hẹn nói chuyện sau. |

### 2.4. Họp online

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| Can you hear me okay? / Can everyone hear me? | Mọi người nghe rõ không? |
| I think you're on mute. | Hình như bạn đang tắt mic. |
| Sorry, I was on mute. | Xin lỗi, tôi quên bật mic. |
| Your audio is a bit choppy. | Âm thanh của bạn hơi bị giật. |
| You froze for a second. | Bạn bị đứng hình một chút. |
| Can you see my screen? | Mọi người thấy màn hình tôi không? |
| Let me share my screen. | Để tôi chia sẻ màn hình. |
| Could you zoom in a bit? | Bạn phóng to lên chút được không? |
| Sorry to interrupt, but ... | Xin lỗi ngắt lời, nhưng ... |
| Can I add something here? | Tôi bổ sung một chút được không? |
| Sorry, go ahead. / After you. | Xin lỗi, bạn nói trước đi. (khi hai người cùng nói) |
| I'll drop the link in the chat. | Tôi sẽ thả link vào khung chat. |
| Let's take this offline. | Để chuyện này bàn riêng sau. |
| I have a hard stop at 4. | Tôi phải rời đúng 4 giờ. |
| Let's wrap up. / To sum up, ... | Mình chốt lại nhé. / Tóm lại, ... |
| What are the next steps? | Các bước tiếp theo là gì? |

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Đặt lịch họp với đồng nghiệp ở múi giờ khác

```
Emma: Hi Nhat, do you have time this week to go through the API design?
Nhat: Sure. Are you available on Wednesday?
Emma: Wednesday is a bit busy. How about Thursday?
Nhat: Thursday works for me. What time is good for you?
Emma: Could we do 10 a.m.? I'm in London, so that's 10 a.m. UK time.
Nhat: Hmm, that's 4 p.m. here in Vietnam. That's fine for me.
Emma: Great. How long do you think we need?
Nhat: Let's say forty-five minutes. There are quite a lot of endpoints.
Emma: Okay. Should we invite Mark from the frontend team too?
Nhat: Good idea. He needs to know about the new response format.
Emma: Perfect. I'll send a calendar invite with a Zoom link.
Nhat: Thanks. Could you also attach the design document?
Emma: Of course. See you on Thursday.
Nhat: See you then.
```

Ghi chú: *go through* — xem qua, rà soát; *Thursday works for me* — thứ 5 được đấy; *See you then* — hẹn gặp lúc đó.

### Hội thoại 2 — Cuộc gọi đầu tiên với recruiter

```
Recruiter: Hello, is this Nhat?
Nhat:      Yes, this is Nhat speaking.
Recruiter: Hi Nhat, this is Jessica from TechHire. I'm calling about the
           Senior Backend Engineer position you applied for. Is this a good
           time to talk?
Nhat:      Hi Jessica. Actually, I'm in a meeting right now. Could I call you
           back in about thirty minutes?
Recruiter: Sure, no problem.
...
Nhat:      Hi Jessica, it's Nhat. Sorry about earlier.
Recruiter: No worries! So, I just have a few questions. First, can you tell me
           a little about your current role?
Nhat:      Sure. I'm a senior backend engineer at a fintech company. I mainly
           work with Java and Spring Boot, and I'm responsible for our payment
           service.
Recruiter: Great. And what's your notice period?
Nhat:      Sorry, could you say that again?
Recruiter: Your notice period. How soon could you start a new job?
Nhat:      Ah, I see. It's thirty days.
Recruiter: Perfect. The next step is a technical interview with the team lead.
           Are you free next Tuesday or Wednesday?
Nhat:      Tuesday afternoon works best. Any time after 2 p.m. Vietnam time.
Recruiter: Okay, I'll check with the team and send you an email to confirm.
Nhat:      Thank you. Could you also tell me who will interview me?
Recruiter: It'll be Daniel, the engineering manager. I'll put the details in
           the email.
Nhat:      Great. Thanks for calling, Jessica. Talk to you soon.
```

Ghi chú: *notice period* — thời gian báo trước khi nghỉ việc; *No worries!* — Không sao!; *confirm* — xác nhận.

### Hội thoại 3 — Sự cố trong buổi họp online

```
Mark: Okay, let's get started. Nhat, are you there?
Nhat: ...
Mark: Nhat, I think you're on mute.
Nhat: Sorry, I was on mute. Can you hear me now?
Mark: Yes, loud and clear. Can you show us the demo?
Nhat: Sure. Let me share my screen... Can you see it?
Mark: We can see your desktop, but not the browser.
Nhat: Oh, sorry. One second... How about now?
Mark: Yes, we can see it now. Could you zoom in a little?
Nhat: Sure. So, this is the new search page. When the user types...
Lin:  Sorry to interrupt, but your audio is a bit choppy.
Nhat: Sorry about that. My internet isn't great today. Let me turn off my
      camera. Is that better?
Lin:  Yes, much better. Thanks.
Nhat: Okay, so when the user types a keyword, the results update immediately.
Mark: Looks great. I have a question about pagination, but I think we can
      take it offline.
Nhat: Sure. I'll drop the link to the ticket in the chat.
Mark: Thanks. Let's wrap up. Next steps: Nhat fixes the filter bug, Lin
      updates the design. Same time next week?
Nhat: Sounds good. Thanks, everyone.
```

Ghi chú: *loud and clear* — nghe rõ; *choppy* — giật, ngắt quãng; *take it offline* — bàn riêng sau buổi họp.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Giới từ thời gian at / on / in + nói lịch hẹn tương lai

| Giới từ | Dùng với | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at** | giờ cụ thể, thời điểm | at 3 p.m., at noon, at the end of the day, at the weekend (Anh) |
| **on** | thứ, ngày cụ thể | on Monday, on May 5th, on Friday morning, on the weekend (Mỹ) |
| **in** | tháng, năm, buổi, khoảng thời gian | in May, in 2027, in the morning, in two hours (= 2 tiếng nữa) |
| (không giới từ) | next / last / this / every / tomorrow | next Tuesday, this afternoon, tomorrow morning |

Lỗi hay gặp: ✗ *on next Monday* → ✓ *next Monday*; ✗ *in Friday* → ✓ *on Friday*; ✗ *at the morning* → ✓ *in the morning*.

**Nói về lịch hẹn tương lai:**
- Đã sắp xếp chắc chắn → **hiện tại tiếp diễn**: I'**m meeting** the client on Thursday. We'**re having** a demo at 10.
- Quyết định ngay lúc nói / hứa → **will**: Okay, I'**ll send** you the invite now.
- Kế hoạch, dự định → **be going to**: I'**m going to** ask for a reschedule.

### Bài tập

Điền at / on / in / (không có).

1. The stand-up is ___ 9:30 ___ the morning.
2. Can we meet ___ Wednesday afternoon?
3. I'll call you back ___ ten minutes.
4. Let's have the demo ___ next Friday.
5. Chọn đúng: Recruiter: "Can you do a call tomorrow?" — You: "Sorry, I (meet / 'm meeting) my manager tomorrow, but I ('ll check / 'm checking) my calendar and email you."

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

Một số câu là **tình huống đóng vai** — tự nói to như đang thật.

1. Role-play: Call your colleague and set up a meeting for next week. Mention the time zone.
2. Role-play: You need to cancel tomorrow's meeting. Apologize and suggest a new time.
3. Role-play: You're five minutes late for an online meeting. What do you say when you join?
4. Role-play: A recruiter calls you while you're busy. What do you say?
5. Role-play: During your demo, nobody can see your screen. What do you say?
6. What do you find most difficult about online meetings in English?
7. How many meetings do you have in a week? Are they useful?
8. How do you deal with time zone differences in your team?
9. Do you prefer phone calls, video calls or messages? Why?
10. What makes a meeting good or bad, in your opinion?
11. How do you prepare for an important call?
12. What would you do if you couldn't understand someone on a call?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q2. Cancel tomorrow's meeting.**

> Hi Sarah, I'm really sorry, but something urgent came up, and I won't be able to make our meeting tomorrow at 10. Would it be possible to move it to Thursday? I'm free any time after 2 p.m. Vietnam time, which is 8 a.m. for you. If that doesn't work, just let me know a time that suits you, and I'll do my best to make it.

**Q6. What do you find most difficult about online meetings in English?**

> For me, the hardest part is when several people speak at the same time, or when someone has a strong accent and their audio is choppy. Sometimes I'm afraid to ask them to repeat. Now I try to say, "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you say it again?" Most people don't mind. I also ask someone to put important things in the chat, like dates and links.

**Q8. How do you deal with time zone differences?**

> Our team is in Vietnam, Germany and the US, so it's tricky. We have one shared meeting time, which is late afternoon for us and morning for Germany. The US team joins only once a week. For everything else, we use async communication: we write clear updates in Slack and record short videos when we need to explain something.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói) — đóng vai recruiter

```
Let's do a role-play. You are a recruiter called Jessica from an
international tech company. You are calling me for a first screening call
for a Senior Backend Engineer role. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer at
about A2-B1 level.

Please:
1. Start the call naturally ("Hi, is this Nhat?"). At first, I will say I'm
   busy and ask to call back. Then we continue.
2. Ask me about my current role, my experience, my notice period, my
   expected salary and when I'm available for a technical interview.
3. Use real recruiter language, but speak a little slowly. Use time zones
   when we schedule the next interview.
4. Once or twice, speak a bit faster or use a word I may not know, so I can
   practice asking you to repeat or explain.
5. After the call, give me feedback: phrases that sounded professional,
   mistakes with at/on/in or future tenses, and a better version of my
   weakest answer.
```

Biến thể: "Now let's role-play an online team meeting. You are the project manager. Create some technical problems: I'm on mute, my screen isn't shared, your audio is choppy."

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: đóng vai đặt lịch họp với khách hàng ở Úc, có đổi lịch giữa chừng.
- Buổi 2: đóng vai họp online, mình là người dẫn (mở đầu, chia sẻ màn hình, chốt next steps).
- Hỏi gia sư: "Which phrases sound too formal or too casual for a meeting with a client?"

## 9. Đáp án

Bài tập ngữ pháp (mục 5):

1. The stand-up is **at** 9:30 **in** the morning.
2. Can we meet **on** Wednesday afternoon?
3. I'll call you back **in** ten minutes.
4. Let's have the demo **(không có giới từ)** next Friday.
5. "Sorry, I**'m meeting** my manager tomorrow (đã sắp xếp), but I**'ll check** my calendar and email you (quyết định ngay lúc nói)."
