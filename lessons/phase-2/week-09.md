# Tuần 9 — Giới thiệu bản thân, gia đình

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-9--giới-thiệu-bản-thân-gia-đình). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Thuộc lòng 5 câu "cứu hộ" và dùng được ngay khi không nghe kịp.
- Tự giới thiệu bản thân trong 1–2 phút: tên, tuổi, quê, nơi sống, công việc, gia đình.
- Hỏi lại người kia được ít nhất 5 câu để giữ cuộc trò chuyện (không chỉ trả lời).
- Dùng đúng thì hiện tại đơn: **he works / she lives / my brother has** (không quên **-s**).
- Hoàn thành buổi gia sư thử (trial) đầu tiên.

## 2. Mẫu câu chính

### 2.1. Câu cứu hộ (học thuộc trước tiên)

| Câu | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Could you say that again, please? | Bạn nói lại được không? | Không nghe kịp |
| Sorry, could you speak a bit more slowly? | Bạn nói chậm hơn chút được không? | Người kia nói quá nhanh |
| What do you mean by "...”? | "..." nghĩa là gì? | Gặp từ lạ |
| Let me think about that for a second. | Để tôi nghĩ một chút. | Cần thời gian nghĩ, tránh im lặng |
| How do you say "..." in English? | "..." tiếng Anh nói thế nào? | Không biết từ, nói từ tiếng Việt hoặc mô tả |
| Sorry, I didn't catch that. | Xin lỗi, tôi chưa nghe rõ. | Cách nói tự nhiên hơn "I don't understand" |
| Do you mean ...? | Ý bạn là ... phải không? | Kiểm tra mình hiểu đúng chưa |

### 2.2. Chào hỏi, mở đầu

| Câu | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hi, I'm Nhat. Nice to meet you. | Chào, tôi là Nhật. Rất vui được gặp. | Lần đầu gặp |
| Nice to meet you too. | Tôi cũng rất vui được gặp bạn. | Đáp lại |
| You can call me Nhat. | Bạn cứ gọi tôi là Nhật. | Khi tên đầy đủ khó đọc |
| How's it going? / How are you doing? | Dạo này thế nào? | Chào thân mật |
| Pretty good, thanks. And you? | Khá ổn, cảm ơn. Còn bạn? | Đáp lại, luôn hỏi lại |
| Where are you based? | Bạn đang ở/làm việc ở đâu? | Hỏi nơi sống (hay dùng với gia sư) |

| Câu | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

Cách shadowing: đọc to theo từng dòng, sau đó che phần tiếng Việt và nói lại. Ghi âm bản của bạn rồi so với bản mẫu (dùng AI đọc lại, hoặc nhờ gia sư đọc).

### Hội thoại 1 — Buổi gia sư đầu tiên

```
Tutor: Hi there! Can you hear me okay?
Nhat:  Yes, I can hear you clearly. Hi, nice to meet you.
Tutor: Nice to meet you too. I'm Sarah. What's your name?
Nhat:  My name is Nguyen Minh Nhat, but you can call me Nhat.
Tutor: Nhat. Did I say that right?
Nhat:  Yes, that's close enough. Thank you.
Tutor: Great. So, where are you from, Nhat?
Nhat:  I'm from Vietnam. I live in Ho Chi Minh City, but I grew up in Quang Nam.
Tutor: Oh, nice. And what do you do?
Nhat:  I'm a software engineer. I work for a company that builds payment software.
Tutor: That sounds interesting. Why are you learning English?
Nhat:  I want to work in an international team, and maybe find a job abroad later.
Tutor: That's a great goal. How long have you been studying?
Nhat:  Sorry, could you say that again, please?
Tutor: Sure. How long have you been studying English?
Nhat:  Oh, I studied at school, but I stopped for many years. I started again two months ago.
Tutor: Well, your English is already quite clear. Let's start, then!
Nhat:  Thank you. Where are you based, Sarah?
Tutor: I'm in Manchester, in the UK.
```

Ghi chú:
- *Can you hear me okay?* — câu kiểm tra âm thanh đầu buổi online.
- *That's close enough.* — "Cũng gần đúng rồi" (khi người khác đọc tên mình).
- *Where are you based?* — hỏi nơi sống/làm việc, tự nhiên hơn "Where do you live?".

### Hội thoại 2 — Nói về gia đình

```
Sarah: Tell me a little about your family.
Nhat:  Sure. I come from a family of five. I have two younger sisters.
Sarah: So you're the oldest?
Nhat:  Yes, I'm the oldest. My first sister is a teacher, and my youngest sister is still at university.
Sarah: What does she study?
Nhat:  She studies accounting. She's in her final year.
Sarah: And your parents? What do they do?
Nhat:  My father was a farmer, but he's retired now. My mother sells vegetables at the local market.
Sarah: Do you see them often?
Nhat:  Not very often. I visit them two or three times a year, usually during Tet.
Sarah: What's Tet?
Nhat:  It's the Lunar New Year. It's the biggest holiday in Vietnam.
Sarah: Oh, like Chinese New Year?
Nhat:  Yes, exactly. Families get together, eat special food and visit relatives.
Sarah: That sounds lovely. Are you married?
Nhat:  Yes, I'm married. My wife is a designer, and we have a son. He's three.
Sarah: Aw, that's a fun age!
Nhat:  Yes, he's very active. He doesn't let us sleep much!
```

Ghi chú:
- *is still at university* — vẫn đang học đại học.
- *in her final year* — năm cuối.
- *get together* — tụ họp.
- *He doesn't let us sleep much* — "Con không cho bọn tôi ngủ mấy" (câu đùa nhẹ, làm hội thoại tự nhiên).

### Hội thoại 3 — Gặp đồng nghiệp mới trong team quốc tế

```
Tom:  Hey, you must be Nhat. I'm Tom, I'm on the backend team.
Nhat: Hi Tom, nice to meet you. Yes, I joined last Monday.
Tom:  Welcome aboard! How's your first week going?
Nhat: Pretty good, thanks. Everyone is very friendly.
Tom:  Where are you working from?
Nhat: I'm working from Ho Chi Minh City. What about you?
Tom:  I'm in Berlin. So we have a five-hour time difference.
Nhat: Sorry, did you say five or nine?
Tom:  Five. Five hours.
Nhat: Ah, okay. So my afternoon is your morning.
Tom:  Exactly. What did you do before this job?
Nhat: I worked at an outsourcing company for six years, mainly on Java projects.
Tom:  Nice. We use a lot of Java here too. Do you have any hobbies?
Nhat: I like running. I usually run in the park on weekends.
Tom:  Cool, me too. Maybe we can compare running apps sometime.
Nhat: Sure, that sounds fun.
```

Ghi chú: *Welcome aboard!* — "Chào mừng gia nhập!"; *time difference* — chênh lệch múi giờ; *sometime* — lúc nào đó.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Thì hiện tại đơn (ngôi thứ 3 số ít)

Người Việt hay quên **-s/-es** khi nói về người thứ ba (bố, mẹ, anh, chị, he, she). Khi nói về gia đình bạn sẽ dùng nó liên tục.

| Chủ ngữ | Khẳng định | Phủ định | Câu hỏi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they | I **live** in Hanoi. | I **don't live** in Hanoi. | **Do** you **live** in Hanoi? |
| he / she / it / my father | He **lives** in Hanoi. | He **doesn't live** in Hanoi. | **Does** he **live** in Hanoi? |

Quy tắc thêm -s:
- Thông thường: work → work**s**, live → live**s**
- Tận cùng -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: watch → watch**es**, go → go**es**, do → do**es**
- Phụ âm + y: study → stud**ies**; nguyên âm + y: play → play**s**
- Bất quy tắc: have → **has**

Ví dụ:
- My sister **teaches** math at a high school.
- My father **doesn't work** anymore. He **is** retired.
- **Does** your brother **live** with your parents?
- My mom **has** a small garden.

Phát âm -s: /s/ sau âm vô thanh (works /wɝːks/), /z/ sau âm hữu thanh (lives /lɪvz/), /ɪz/ sau s, sh, ch (watches /ˈwɑːtʃɪz/). Nói rõ âm cuối!

### Bài tập

Chia động từ trong ngoặc.

1. My brother ___ (work) in Singapore.
2. My mother ___ (not / like) big cities.
3. ___ your sister ___ (have) any kids?
4. She ___ (study) English every evening.
5. My parents ___ (live) in Nghe An, and my grandmother ___ (live) with them.

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

Trả lời mỗi câu 30–60 giây, nói to, ghi âm ít nhất 3 câu.

1. Can you introduce yourself in one minute?
2. Where are you from originally? What is it famous for?
3. Where do you live now? Do you like it there? Why?
4. Tell me about your family.
5. Who are you closest to in your family?
6. Do you take after your mother or your father? In what way?
7. What do your parents do?
8. How often do you see your family?
9. What do you do for a living?
10. Why are you learning English?
11. What do you usually do in your free time?
12. What is the best thing about your hometown?
13. How is your family different from a typical family in your country?
14. What are your goals for the next year?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q1. Can you introduce yourself in one minute?**

> Sure. My name is Nhat, and I'm thirty-two years old. I'm originally from Quang Nam, a province in central Vietnam, but I've lived in Ho Chi Minh City for about ten years. I'm a software engineer. I mostly work on backend systems with Java and Spring Boot. I'm married, and we have a little son. In my free time, I like running and reading tech blogs. I'm learning English again because I want to work in an international team and communicate with my colleagues more confidently.

**Q4. Tell me about your family.**

> I come from a family of four: my parents, my younger sister and me. My father is retired, and my mother still runs a small grocery store in my hometown. My sister works as a nurse in Da Nang. We're quite close, but we don't see each other very often because we live in different cities. We usually get together during Tet, and we video call every weekend.

**Q6. Do you take after your mother or your father?**

> I think I take after my father. We both are... sorry, we are both quite quiet, and we like fixing things. When I was a kid, I always watched him repair the radio or the motorbike. Maybe that's why I became an engineer.

(Chú ý: tự sửa lỗi giữa câu như "sorry, we are both..." là bình thường và tự nhiên.)

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
You are a friendly English tutor. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer, and my
English level is about A2. This week's topic is "introducing yourself and your family".

Please:
1. Act like we are meeting for the first time. Ask me one question at a time.
2. Use simple, natural English and speak a little slowly.
3. Ask follow-up questions about my answers, like a real conversation.
4. After each of my answers, if I made a mistake, tell me the correct sentence
   in one short line (especially missing -s, wrong tense, missing articles).
   Then continue the conversation.
5. After about 10 minutes, give me a short summary: my 3 most common mistakes
   and 5 useful phrases I should learn.
Let's start.
```

### Chuẩn bị cho buổi gia sư (trial)

Nói với gia sư ngay đầu buổi:
- "This is my first lesson. My level is about A2. I'm a software engineer, and my goal is to speak in IT job interviews in about seven months."
- "Please correct my mistakes, especially grammar and pronunciation. You can type corrections in the chat."
- "Please speak a little slowly at first."

5 câu hỏi để hỏi gia sư:
1. Where are you based, and how long have you been teaching?
2. What do you think my biggest problem is after listening to me today?
3. How can I improve my pronunciation of final sounds?
4. What should I practice before our next lesson?
5. Do you have experience teaching people who work in IT?

Cuối buổi, nhờ: "Could you send me the list of corrections from today?"
