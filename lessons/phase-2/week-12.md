# Tuần 12 — Công việc, công ty

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-12--công-việc-công-ty). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Trả lời "What do you do?" trong 2–5 phút: vai trò, công ty, sản phẩm, team, trách nhiệm, công nghệ.
- Mô tả được loại công ty: outsourcing, product, startup, corporation; khách hàng ở đâu; quy mô.
- Phân biệt **hiện tại đơn** (việc thường làm) và **hiện tại tiếp diễn** (việc đang làm dạo này): *I work on payments. Right now I'm working on a refund feature.*
- Chịu được câu hỏi đào sâu: "What exactly are you responsible for?", "What's the hardest part?"

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — "What do you do?" với gia sư

```
Tutor: So, what do you do, Nhat?
Nhat:  I'm a software engineer. I work as a backend developer at an
       outsourcing company in Ho Chi Minh City.
Tutor: Outsourcing... so you build software for other companies?
Nhat:  Exactly. Our clients are mostly in Japan and Australia. They send us
       requirements, and we build and maintain their systems.
Tutor: What kind of systems?
Nhat:  My current project is a logistics system for an Australian company.
       It tracks deliveries and manages warehouses.
Tutor: Interesting. And what's your role in the project?
Nhat:  I'm a senior developer. I'm responsible for the order service, and I
       also review code for three junior developers.
Tutor: How big is your team?
Nhat:  There are ten people: six developers, two testers, a business analyst
       and a project manager.
Tutor: What do you like most about your job?
Nhat:  I like that I can learn many different technologies. Every project is
       different.
Tutor: And what's the most difficult part?
Nhat:  Let me think... Probably communication. The client changes
       requirements quite often, and sometimes it's hard to explain technical
       problems in English.
Tutor: Well, that's why you're here!
Nhat:  Exactly!
```

### Hội thoại 2 — AI/đồng nghiệp hỏi sâu về công việc hiện tại

```
Kate: What are you working on at the moment?
Nhat: Right now, we're moving our payment system from a monolith to
      microservices.
Kate: Sounds like a big project. Why are you doing that?
Nhat: The old system is hard to scale. When we have a big sale, it slows down
      a lot.
Kate: What's your part in it?
Nhat: I'm building the refund service. I'm also writing the integration tests.
Kate: How's it going so far?
Nhat: It's going okay, but slower than we planned. The old code doesn't have
      many tests, so we need to be careful.
Kate: When do you plan to finish?
Nhat: We're aiming to go live by the end of the year.
Kate: Who do you work with most?
Nhat: Mostly the other backend developers and the DevOps engineer. We have a
      sync meeting every Wednesday.
Kate: Do you enjoy it?
Nhat: Yes, I do. It's challenging, but I'm learning a lot about system design.
```

Ghi chú: *monolith* — hệ thống nguyên khối; *go live* — đưa lên chạy thật; *aiming to* — đặt mục tiêu; *so far* — tính đến giờ.

### Hội thoại 3 — Small talk ở sự kiện công nghệ

```
Raj:  Hi, I'm Raj. Which company are you from?
Nhat: Hi Raj, I'm Nhat. I work for a small fintech startup called PayNow.
Raj:  Oh, I haven't heard of it. What do you guys do?
Nhat: We have a mobile wallet app. People use it to pay bills and send money.
Raj:  Cool. How many people work there?
Nhat: About sixty. Around half of them are engineers.
Raj:  And what's your role?
Nhat: I'm a backend engineer. I mostly work on the transaction system.
Raj:  Nice. What's it like working at a startup?
Nhat: It's fast and a bit chaotic, but I have a lot of freedom. What about you?
Raj:  I'm a data engineer at a bank. Much slower, but very stable.
Nhat: I guess both have pros and cons.
Raj:  Definitely.
```

Ghi chú: *What do you guys do?* — Công ty bạn làm gì? (thân mật); *chaotic* /keɪˈɑːtɪk/ — hỗn loạn; *pros and cons* — ưu và nhược điểm.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Hiện tại đơn vs Hiện tại tiếp diễn

| | Hiện tại đơn | Hiện tại tiếp diễn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dùng cho | Việc thường xuyên, sự thật, công việc lâu dài | Việc đang diễn ra lúc này / giai đoạn này, tạm thời |
| Ví dụ | I **work** for a bank. | I'**m working** on a new mobile app **this month**. |
| | Our team **uses** Java. | We'**re** currently **moving** to Kotlin. |
| | She **manages** the QA team. | She'**s managing** the release **this week** because the lead is on leave. |
| Từ đi kèm | usually, every day, always | now, at the moment, currently, these days, this week |

Động từ trạng thái **không dùng tiếp diễn**: know, understand, like, want, need, believe, belong, mean.
- ✗ I'm knowing Python. → ✓ I **know** Python.
- ✗ I'm wanting to change jobs. → ✓ I **want** to change jobs.

Mẫu trả lời phỏng vấn kết hợp cả 2: *"I **work** as a backend developer, and I **build** APIs for our mobile app. **At the moment**, I'**m working** on a new search feature."*

### Bài tập

1. I usually ___ (write) unit tests before I push my code.
2. Right now, our team ___ (prepare) for a big release.
3. She ___ (know) a lot about Kubernetes.
4. We ___ (use) Jira for task tracking, but this quarter we ___ (try) Linear.
5. Sửa lỗi: *I am work at a startup, and currently I build a chatbot.*

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

1. What do you do?
2. What does your company do? Who are your clients or users?
3. How long have you been working there? What did you do before?
4. Describe your team. Who do you work with most?
5. What are you responsible for?
6. What are you working on at the moment?
7. What does a typical sprint look like on your team?
8. What do you like most about your job?
9. What is the most challenging part of your job?
10. What's the difference between working at an outsourcing company and a product company?
11. What technologies do you use every day?
12. Where do you see yourself in three years?
13. Why do you want to change jobs? (hoặc: Why do you like your current job?)
14. What would your perfect company look like?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q1. What do you do?**

> I'm a senior backend engineer. I've been working in software for about eight years, and I've been at my current company for three. It's an outsourcing company, and our main clients are in Japan. Right now I'm on a project for a Japanese retail company. We're building an inventory management system. I'm responsible for the order service and the integration with their warehouse system. I also review code and help two junior developers on the team.

**Q9. What is the most challenging part of your job?**

> For me, the most challenging part is working with changing requirements. Our client often changes their mind in the middle of a sprint. So I try to ask a lot of questions at the beginning, and I write down the decisions in the ticket. That way, if something changes, everyone knows why.

**Q10. Outsourcing vs product company?**

> In an outsourcing company, you work on many different projects for different clients, so you learn many technologies quickly. But you don't always own the product, and the client makes most of the decisions. In a product company, you work on one product for a long time. You can see the impact of your work on real users, and you're more involved in decisions. Right now, I'd prefer a product company because I want to go deeper into one domain.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
You are a curious friend who works in tech in another country. I'm a
Vietnamese software engineer at about A2-B1 level. This week's topic is
"my job and my company".

Please:
1. Start with "So, what do you do?" and then dig deeper. Ask about my company,
   clients or users, my team, my responsibilities, what I'm working on now,
   and what I like and dislike about my job.
2. Ask "why" and "how" follow-up questions, like an interested person would.
   Ask one question at a time.
3. If I mix up present simple and present continuous (I work / I'm working),
   or say "I'm knowing", correct me in one line, then continue.
4. If I don't know a word, help me with it and ask me to say the sentence again.
5. At the end, write a better version of my answer to "What do you do?"
   in about 80 words, using my own information.
```

Ngày T5 (AI hỏi sâu): thêm vào cuối prompt "Now act as a recruiter. Ask me harder questions, like 'What exactly did YOU do in that project?'".

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: nói 3 phút "What do you do?" không ngắt, sau đó nhờ gia sư hỏi 5 câu hỏi tiếp theo.
- Buổi 2: giải thích công ty bạn cho người không làm IT: "Imagine I'm your grandmother. Explain what your company does."
- Nhờ gia sư: "Could you help me write a 1-minute version of my job description? I want to reuse it in interviews."
- Lưu bản 1 phút này vào `interview/answers.md`.
