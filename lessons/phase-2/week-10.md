# Tuần 10 — Thói quen hằng ngày

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-10--thói-quen-hằng-ngày). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Kể một ngày bình thường (ngày làm việc và cuối tuần) trong 2 phút, theo đúng thứ tự thời gian.
- Dùng tự nhiên 15+ cụm động từ về sinh hoạt: *wake up, get up, get to work, catch up on, wind down*...
- Dùng đúng trạng từ tần suất (*always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never*) và đặt đúng vị trí.
- Nói về thói quen làm việc: daily stand-up, code review, deep work, họp với khách hàng.

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Một ngày làm việc

```
Tutor: So, Nhat, what does a typical day look like for you?
Nhat:  Well, I usually wake up at six thirty. I'm not really a morning person,
       so I need a strong coffee first.
Tutor: Vietnamese coffee?
Nhat:  Of course. Iced coffee with condensed milk.
Tutor: Ha! And then?
Nhat:  Then I take my son to kindergarten, and I ride my motorbike to the office.
Tutor: How long does it take you?
Nhat:  About forty minutes, because the traffic is terrible in the morning.
Tutor: Forty minutes! That's a long time.
Nhat:  Yes, so I often listen to English podcasts on the way.
Tutor: That's a smart idea. What do you do when you get to the office?
Nhat:  First, I check my messages. Then we have a stand-up meeting at nine thirty.
Tutor: What happens in a stand-up?
Nhat:  Everyone says what they did yesterday, what they're doing today,
       and if they have any blockers.
Tutor: I see. And what do you do after that?
Nhat:  I spend most of the morning coding. In the afternoon, I usually review
       my teammates' code.
Tutor: What time do you finish?
Nhat:  Usually around six, but sometimes later if there's an urgent bug.
```

Ghi chú:
- *condensed milk* — sữa đặc; *kindergarten* — mẫu giáo.
- *on the way* — trên đường đi.
- *blockers* — vướng mắc làm mình không làm tiếp được (từ rất hay dùng trong team Agile).

### Hội thoại 2 — Buổi tối và cuối tuần

```
Lisa: Are you a morning person or a night owl?
Nhat: Definitely a night owl. My brain works better at night.
Lisa: So what do you do in the evenings?
Nhat: When I get home, I have dinner with my family. Then I play with my son
      for a bit.
Lisa: That's nice. Do you ever work in the evening?
Nhat: Sometimes. We work with a client in the US, so I occasionally have
      meetings at ten p.m.
Lisa: Oh, that sounds tiring.
Nhat: It is, but it doesn't happen very often. Maybe once or twice a month.
Lisa: What about weekends? What do you usually do?
Nhat: On Saturdays, I sleep in until about eight. Then I catch up on
      housework and go grocery shopping with my wife.
Lisa: And Sundays?
Nhat: Sundays are family days. We often visit my wife's parents and have lunch
      together.
Lisa: Do you ever have time for yourself?
Nhat: Not much, to be honest. But I always go running on Sunday evening.
      It helps me clear my head.
Lisa: Good for you!
```

Ghi chú: *sleep in* — ngủ nướng; *to be honest* — nói thật thì; *clear my head* — làm đầu óc thoải mái.

### Hội thoại 3 — Thói quen làm việc (với đồng nghiệp nước ngoài)

```
Anna: How do you organize your day? You always seem so productive.
Nhat: Thanks! I try to do the hardest task first, in the morning.
Anna: Do you turn off notifications?
Nhat: Yes, I usually turn off Slack for two hours. I tell the team I'm in
      focus time.
Anna: Good idea. I get distracted all the time.
Nhat: Me too, before. Now I also write a short to-do list every morning.
Anna: On paper or in an app?
Nhat: In a notes app. I check things off during the day. It feels good.
Anna: And when do you answer messages?
Nhat: Usually after lunch. I reply to everything at once.
Anna: That makes sense. I might try that.
Nhat: Let me know if it works for you.
```

Ghi chú: *productive* — năng suất; *get distracted* — bị xao nhãng; *check things off* — đánh dấu xong việc; *That makes sense* — nghe hợp lý.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Trạng từ tần suất và vị trí

| Trạng từ | Khoảng % | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| always | 100% | I always check my code before pushing. |
| usually | ~90% | I usually start work at nine. |
| often | ~70% | I often work late on Fridays. |
| sometimes | ~50% | I sometimes work from a coffee shop. |
| rarely / seldom | ~10% | I rarely take a day off. |
| never | 0% | I never skip breakfast. |

**Vị trí:**
- Đứng **trước động từ thường**: I **usually** wake up at six.
- Đứng **sau động từ "to be"**: I'm **always** tired on Mondays.
- Với trợ động từ: I **don't usually** work on weekends. / I **have never** been late.
- *Sometimes* và *usually* có thể đứng đầu câu: **Sometimes** I work late.

**Cụm tần suất** (đứng cuối câu): every day, once a week, twice a month, three times a year, on weekdays, on Mondays.

- I go running **three times a week**.
- We have a retrospective **every two weeks**.

**Câu hỏi:** How often do you ...? — *How often do you work from home?* — *About twice a week.*

### Bài tập

Sắp xếp lại / sửa câu cho đúng.

1. I go usually to work by bus. → ___
2. She late is never for meetings. → ___
3. (sometimes) We have lunch in the office. → viết lại câu, thêm *sometimes* đúng vị trí.
4. Trả lời: *How often do you have a retrospective?* (mỗi 2 tuần) → ___
5. I don't work often on Sundays. → ___

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

1. What does a typical weekday look like for you?
2. What time do you usually wake up? Is it easy for you?
3. Are you a morning person or a night owl?
4. How do you get to work? How long does it take?
5. What do you usually do first when you start work?
6. How many meetings do you have on a normal day?
7. When do you feel most productive?
8. What distracts you the most at work? How do you deal with it?
9. What do you usually do after work?
10. How is your weekend routine different from your weekday routine?
11. Do you prefer working from home or from the office? Why?
12. What is one habit you want to change?
13. How often do you exercise?
14. What does a typical day look like for a developer in Vietnam?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q1. What does a typical weekday look like for you?**

> On weekdays, I usually get up at about six thirty. I make coffee and get ready, then I take my daughter to school. I get to the office at around eight forty-five. The first thing I do is check Slack and my emails. At nine thirty, we have a short stand-up. After that, I spend most of the morning coding, because that's when I can focus best. I have lunch with my teammates and sometimes take a short nap. In the afternoon, I review pull requests and attend meetings. I usually finish at six and get home at about six forty-five.

**Q8. What distracts you the most at work?**

> Honestly, notifications. Slack messages come in all the time, and I want to answer them immediately. So now I turn off notifications for two hours every morning and tell my team I'm in focus time. If something is really urgent, they can call me.

**Q12. What is one habit you want to change?**

> I want to stop checking my phone right after I wake up. I usually read the news or social media in bed for twenty minutes, and then I'm in a hurry. I want to use that time to listen to English instead.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
You are my English conversation partner. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer
at about A2 level. This week's topic is "daily routines and work habits".

Please:
1. Ask me about my typical weekday, my weekend, and how I organize my work.
   Ask one question at a time and follow up on what I say.
2. Use phrasal verbs like "wake up", "get to work", "catch up on", "wind down"
   in your questions so I can hear them used naturally.
3. When I make a mistake with frequency adverbs (always, usually, often...)
   or forget the -s on verbs, correct me briefly, then continue.
4. Once during the conversation, ask me to describe my day again in two
   minutes without stopping. Then tell me what was good and what to improve.
5. At the end, list 5 phrases I used well and 3 mistakes I should fix.
```

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: "Can we talk about daily routines? I'd like to describe my day, and please stop me when I make a mistake."
- Buổi 2: Đọc bản ghi âm "một ngày làm việc của tôi" (đã viết sẵn) cho gia sư nghe, nhờ: "Could you rewrite this to sound more natural?"
- Hỏi gia sư: "What's a typical day like for you?" — để luyện nghe và hỏi tiếp.
- Hỏi: "What's the difference between *wake up* and *get up*?" và "Do people say *I go to work by motorbike* or *I ride my motorbike to work*?"
