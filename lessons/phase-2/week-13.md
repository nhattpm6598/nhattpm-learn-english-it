# Tuần 13 — Đưa ra ý kiến

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-13--đưa-ra-ý-kiến). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Nêu ý kiến theo cấu trúc **Ý kiến → Lý do → Ví dụ → Kết luận** (dùng lại cho phỏng vấn sau này).
- Đồng ý / không đồng ý **lịch sự**, không nói "No, you're wrong."
- Dùng câu so sánh (*more flexible than, better for, not as ... as*) để cân nhắc hai lựa chọn.
- Tranh luận nhẹ 5–10 phút về: remote vs office, AI trong lập trình, startup vs tập đoàn.

## 2. Mẫu câu chính

### 2.1. Nêu ý kiến

| Câu | Nghĩa | Mức độ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I think ... / I don't think ... | Tôi nghĩ ... / Tôi không nghĩ ... | Cơ bản. Lưu ý: nói *I don't think it's good*, không nói *I think it's not good* |
| In my opinion, ... | Theo ý tôi, ... | Hơi trang trọng |
| From my experience, ... | Theo kinh nghiệm của tôi, ... | Rất hợp cho phỏng vấn |
| Personally, I prefer ... | Cá nhân tôi thích ... hơn | Nhấn mạnh quan điểm riêng |
| I feel that ... | Tôi cảm thấy rằng ... | Mềm |
| If you ask me, ... | Nếu hỏi tôi thì ... | Thân mật |
| I'm pretty sure that ... | Tôi khá chắc là ... | Tự tin |
| I'm not sure, but I'd say ... | Tôi không chắc, nhưng tôi cho là ... | Khi chưa chắc |
| It depends on ... | Còn tùy vào ... | Câu trả lời an toàn, phải giải thích tiếp |

### 2.2. Đưa lý do, ví dụ, kết luận

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| The main reason is that ... | Lý do chính là ... |
| Another reason is ... | Một lý do khác là ... |
| For example, / For instance, ... | Ví dụ, ... |
| In my last project, ... | Trong dự án gần nhất, ... |
| On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ... | Một mặt ... mặt khác ... |
| That's why I think ... | Đó là lý do tôi nghĩ ... |
| So overall, ... | Vậy nhìn chung, ... |

### 2.3. Đồng ý

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| I agree with you. / I totally agree. | Tôi đồng ý / hoàn toàn đồng ý. |
| That's a good point. | Ý đó hay. |
| Exactly. / Absolutely. | Chính xác. |
| I couldn't agree more. | Tôi hoàn toàn đồng ý. |
| You're right about that. | Bạn nói đúng điểm đó. |

### 2.4. Không đồng ý lịch sự

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| I see your point, but ... | Tôi hiểu ý bạn, nhưng ... |
| That's true, but ... | Đúng vậy, nhưng ... |
| I'm not sure I agree. | Tôi không chắc là tôi đồng ý. |
| I see it a bit differently. | Tôi nhìn nhận hơi khác một chút. |
| I agree up to a point, but ... | Tôi đồng ý một phần, nhưng ... |
| Maybe, but have you thought about ...? | Có thể, nhưng bạn đã nghĩ đến ... chưa? |
| I'm afraid I don't agree. | Tôi e là tôi không đồng ý. |

### 2.5. Hỏi ý kiến người khác, giữ thảo luận

| Câu | Nghĩa |
| --- | --- |
| What do you think? / What's your opinion? | Bạn nghĩ sao? |
| How do you feel about ...? | Bạn thấy ... thế nào? |
| Do you agree? | Bạn có đồng ý không? |
| Why do you say that? | Sao bạn nói vậy? |
| Fair enough. | Cũng hợp lý. (chấp nhận ý người kia) |
| Let's agree to disagree. | Thôi mỗi người một ý vậy. |

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Remote vs office

```
Tutor: So, Nhat, do you prefer working from home or from the office?
Nhat:  Personally, I prefer working from home, at least three days a week.
Tutor: Why is that?
Nhat:  The main reason is the commute. It takes me almost an hour every day.
       At home, I can use that time to sleep a bit more or study English.
Tutor: That makes sense. But don't you feel lonely at home?
Nhat:  Sometimes, yes. That's a good point. I miss having lunch with my
       teammates.
Tutor: Some people say you're less productive at home. What do you think?
Nhat:  I see your point, but from my experience, it's the opposite. At the
       office, people come to my desk all the time. At home, I can focus
       better.
Tutor: But what about new team members? How do they learn?
Nhat:  Hmm, I agree up to a point. For juniors, the office is probably better,
       because they can ask questions more easily.
Tutor: So what's the best solution?
Nhat:  I think hybrid is the best. For example, two days in the office for
       meetings and team activities, and three days at home for deep work.
Tutor: That sounds like a fair compromise.
Nhat:  Yes, so overall, I think flexibility is the most important thing.
```

Ghi chú: *the opposite* — ngược lại; *compromise* /ˈkɑːmprəmaɪz/ — giải pháp dung hòa; *fair* — công bằng, hợp lý.

### Hội thoại 2 — AI có nên dùng khi lập trình không?

```
Leo:  Do you use AI tools when you code?
Nhat: Yes, every day. I think they make me much faster.
Leo:  Really? I'm not sure I agree. I think they make developers lazy.
Nhat: That's true for some people. But in my opinion, it depends on how you
      use them.
Leo:  What do you mean?
Nhat: Well, if you just copy and paste the code without reading it, that's a
      problem. But if you review it carefully, it's like having a fast
      assistant.
Leo:  But what about junior developers? They don't know when the code is wrong.
Nhat: Fair enough. That's a real risk. Juniors should probably write more code
      by themselves first.
Leo:  Exactly. I learned the most when I was stuck for hours on a bug.
Nhat: I see it a bit differently. Being stuck is useful, but being stuck for
      a whole day isn't. AI can explain things faster than searching Google.
Leo:  Hmm. Okay, maybe we just agree to disagree.
Nhat: Sure. But I think we both agree that you must understand the code you
      push.
Leo:  Absolutely.
```

### Hội thoại 3 — Startup hay tập đoàn?

```
Chris: If you could choose, would you work for a startup or a big company?
Nhat:  It's a tough question. On the one hand, startups are more exciting.
       You can try new things and see your impact quickly.
Chris: And on the other hand?
Nhat:  On the other hand, big companies are more stable. The salary is usually
       better, and the processes are clearer.
Chris: So which one?
Nhat:  Right now, with a young family, I'd say a big company. Stability is
       more important for me than excitement.
Chris: That's interesting. I think startups teach you more.
Nhat:  That's a good point. You learn faster because you do everything.
       Maybe in a few years I'll try a startup.
Chris: Never say never!
```

Ghi chú: *tough question* — câu hỏi khó; *impact* — tác động; *Never say never* — đừng bao giờ nói không bao giờ.

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — So sánh hơn, so sánh nhất, "not as ... as"

| Loại tính từ | So sánh hơn | So sánh nhất |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 âm tiết: fast, cheap | fast**er** than | the fast**est** |
| tận cùng -y: easy, busy | eas**ier** than | the eas**iest** |
| 2+ âm tiết: flexible, expensive | **more** flexible than | **the most** flexible |
| bất quy tắc: good, bad, far | better, worse, farther | the best, the worst, the farthest |

**So sánh kém / bằng:**
- Office work is **not as flexible as** remote work.
- Go is **as fast as** Java for this use case.
- Startups are **less stable than** big companies.

**Nhấn mạnh:** **much / a lot / a bit** + so sánh hơn
- Working from home is **much more** comfortable.
- The new API is **a bit slower than** the old one.

Lỗi hay gặp: ✗ *more better*, ✗ *more easier* → ✓ *better*, ✓ *easier*.

### Bài tập

1. Remote work is ___ (flexible) office work.
2. For me, the commute is ___ (bad) part of office work.
3. Python is ___ (easy) to learn ___ C++.
4. Viết lại, nghĩa không đổi: *Big companies are more stable than startups.* → Startups are not ___
5. Sửa lỗi: *I'm agree, hybrid is more better for everyone.*

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

Trả lời theo khung: **Opinion → Reason → Example → Conclusion** (khoảng 45–60 giây mỗi câu).

1. Do you prefer working from home or from the office? Why?
2. Is it better to work for a startup or a big company?
3. Should developers use AI tools to write code?
4. Do you think a university degree is necessary to become a developer?
5. Is it better to be a specialist or a generalist in IT?
6. Are long working hours normal in the IT industry? Is that okay?
7. Should companies let employees choose their own working hours?
8. Is it better to learn English with a tutor or by yourself?
9. Do you think social media is good or bad for young people?
10. Is it a good idea to change jobs every two years?
11. Should every developer write unit tests?
12. Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?
13. What do you think is the most important skill for a software engineer?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q4. Do you think a university degree is necessary to become a developer?**

> In my opinion, it's helpful, but it's not necessary. The main reason is that most of the skills we use every day, like using frameworks or Git, we learn at work, not at university. For example, one of the best developers on my team studied economics. She taught herself programming online. On the other hand, a degree gives you a strong foundation in algorithms and computer systems, and some companies still require it. So overall, I think skills and experience are more important than a degree.

**Q10. Is it a good idea to change jobs every two years?**

> It depends. If you're junior, changing jobs can help you learn faster and get a higher salary. But I don't think it's good to change too often. When you stay longer, you see the long-term result of your decisions, and you can take more responsibility. From my experience, my third and fourth year at a company were when I learned the most. So I'd say two years is a bit short. Three to four years is better.

**Q13. Most important skill?**

> If you ask me, it's communication. Coding is important, of course, but most problems in projects come from misunderstanding, not from bad code. A developer who asks good questions and explains things clearly saves the team a lot of time.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
Let's have a friendly debate. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer at about
A2-B1 level, practicing how to give opinions and disagree politely.

Topic: "Should developers use AI tools to write code?" (or choose another
tech topic if I ask).

Rules:
1. First, ask for my opinion. Then take the OPPOSITE side, even if you don't
   really believe it, and challenge my reasons one at a time.
2. Use simple English. Keep each of your turns under 3 sentences.
3. Push me to give reasons and examples. If I just say "I think so", ask "Why?"
4. If I disagree rudely or say things like "I'm agree", correct me briefly
   and show me a polite version.
5. After 10 minutes, stop and give me feedback: which opinion and
   disagreement phrases I used, and 3 phrases I should try next time.
```

Đổi chủ đề các ngày khác: "Remote work is better than office work", "Every developer should learn system design", "Microservices are overused".

### Với gia sư

- Buổi 1: tranh luận "remote vs office". Nhờ gia sư cố tình đứng phía ngược lại.
- Buổi 2: đưa ra 3 ý kiến về một chủ đề công nghệ, hỏi gia sư: "Does this sound too direct or rude?" — người Việt hay dịch thẳng, nghe cứng.
- Hỏi gia sư: "How do people disagree with their manager politely in your country?"
