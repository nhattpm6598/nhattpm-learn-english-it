# Tuần 11 — Sở thích, cuối tuần

> Checklist hằng ngày: xem [phase-2-conversation.md](../../phase-2-conversation.md#tuần-11--sở-thích-cuối-tuần). File này là **tài liệu học** cho tuần đó.

## 1. Mục tiêu tuần

- Nói thích / không thích ở nhiều mức độ, không chỉ "I like...": *I'm into..., I'm a big fan of..., I can't stand...*
- Kể lại cuối tuần vừa rồi bằng **thì quá khứ đơn**, phát âm rõ đuôi **-ed**.
- Giữ hội thoại: hỏi lại, phản hồi (*Oh really? That sounds fun!*), hỏi thêm chi tiết.
- Trả lời câu "What do you do in your free time?" dài 4–6 câu, không cụt lủn.

## 3. Hội thoại mẫu

### Hội thoại 1 — Hỏi về sở thích

```
Tutor: So, what do you like to do in your free time?
Nhat:  I'm really into running. I run three times a week, usually in the park
       near my house.
Tutor: Oh, nice! How did you get into running?
Nhat:  A few years ago, I had back pain from sitting all day. My doctor told
       me to exercise more, so I started running.
Tutor: Has it helped?
Nhat:  Yes, a lot. And it helps me clear my head after work.
Tutor: Have you ever run a race?
Nhat:  Yes, I ran a half marathon last year in Da Nang.
Tutor: Wow, that's impressive! How long did it take you?
Nhat:  About two hours and ten minutes. Not very fast, but I finished.
Tutor: That's still great. Do you have any other hobbies?
Nhat:  I also like reading, mostly tech books and some novels.
       What about you? What do you do for fun?
Tutor: I love gardening. I grow tomatoes and herbs on my balcony.
Nhat:  That sounds relaxing. Is it difficult?
Tutor: Not really, you just need to water them every day.
Nhat:  Maybe I should try it. My wife loves plants.
```

Ghi chú: *get into* — bắt đầu thích; *half marathon* — bán marathon (21 km); *impressive* — ấn tượng; *herbs* /ɝːbz/ (Mỹ không đọc âm h).

### Hội thoại 2 — Thứ Hai đầu tuần: "How was your weekend?"

```
Mia:  Morning, Nhat! How was your weekend?
Nhat: It was pretty busy, actually. On Saturday, we went to my wife's
      hometown for her cousin's wedding.
Mia:  Oh, a wedding! How was it?
Nhat: It was fun, but very loud. Vietnamese weddings usually have a lot of
      karaoke.
Mia:  Ha! Did you sing?
Nhat: No, I didn't. I'm not a big fan of karaoke. But my wife sang two songs.
Mia:  And on Sunday?
Nhat: We drove back on Sunday morning. It took four hours because of the
      traffic. In the afternoon, I just stayed home and relaxed.
Mia:  Sounds like you needed it.
Nhat: Definitely. What about you? Did you do anything fun?
Mia:  I went hiking with some friends. The weather was perfect.
Nhat: Nice! Where did you go?
Mia:  A national park about an hour from the city. We walked about fifteen
      kilometers.
Nhat: Fifteen! You must be tired today.
Mia:  My legs are a bit sore, yes!
```

Ghi chú: *cousin* — anh chị em họ; *sore* — đau mỏi cơ; *You must be tired* — chắc là bạn mệt lắm (phỏng đoán).

### Hội thoại 3 — Không cùng sở thích, vẫn giữ hội thoại

```
Ben:  Do you play video games?
Nhat: Not really. It's not really my thing. I used to play a lot in
      university, though.
Ben:  What did you play?
Nhat: Mostly Warcraft and some football games. What about you?
Ben:  I'm crazy about strategy games. I play almost every night.
Nhat: Every night? When do you sleep?
Ben:  Ha, not enough! What do you do instead?
Nhat: These days, I spend most of my free time with my son. We go to the park
      or build Lego together.
Ben:  Lego is great. I still have a big box at my parents' house.
Nhat: Then you should come over and help us build a spaceship!
Ben:  Deal!
```

Ghi chú: *though* (cuối câu) — tuy vậy; *come over* — qua nhà chơi; *Deal!* — Chốt!

## 5. Ngữ pháp nhanh — Quá khứ đơn (kể cuối tuần)

**Cấu trúc:**
- Khẳng định: I **went** to the beach. / We **watched** a movie.
- Phủ định: I **didn't go** out. (sau *didn't* là động từ nguyên mẫu — **không** nói "didn't went")
- Câu hỏi: **Did** you **go** anywhere? / What **did** you **do**?
- To be: It **was** fun. They **were** tired. **Was** it crowded?

**Động từ có quy tắc** (thêm -ed), đọc đuôi theo 3 cách:

| Âm cuối | Đọc -ed | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| /t/, /d/ | /ɪd/ | visited, needed, started |
| âm vô thanh (p, k, s, sh, ch, f) | /t/ | watched /wɑːtʃt/, worked, stopped |
| âm hữu thanh còn lại | /d/ | played /pleɪd/, stayed, cleaned |

Lỗi hay gặp: đọc "watched" thành "watch-ed" (2 âm tiết). Đúng là **1 âm tiết**: /wɑːtʃt/.

**Động từ bất quy tắc hay dùng khi kể cuối tuần:** go → went, have → had, eat → ate, see → saw, meet → met, take → took, buy → bought, drive → drove, sleep → slept, spend → spent, swim → swam, get → got, do → did, come → came, sing → sang.

**Nhắc thêm: like/love/enjoy/hate + V-ing** — *I enjoy cooking* (không nói "enjoy to cook"). *Like/love/hate* có thể dùng + to V, nhưng V-ing tự nhiên hơn khi nói sở thích.

### Bài tập

1. Last Saturday, I ___ (go) to the cinema with my wife.
2. We ___ (not / eat) out on Sunday. We ___ (cook) at home.
3. ___ you ___ (meet) your friends last weekend?
4. How ___ the trip? — It ___ (be) great, but the hotel ___ (be) expensive.
5. Sửa lỗi: *I enjoy to play badminton, and yesterday I didn't played because it rained.*

(Đáp án ở mục 9.)

## 6. Câu hỏi luyện nói

1. What do you like to do in your free time?
2. How did you get into your favorite hobby?
3. Is there a hobby you used to have but gave up? Why?
4. What hobby would you like to try in the future?
5. How was your last weekend? Tell me everything.
6. What's your idea of a perfect weekend?
7. Do you prefer staying in or going out on weekends?
8. Is there anything you can't stand doing?
9. Do you have a side project? What is it?
10. What do people in Vietnam usually do on weekends?
11. Do you think hobbies are important for software engineers? Why?
12. What was the best trip you took last year?
13. Are you looking forward to anything this month?

### Câu trả lời mẫu

**Q1. What do you like to do in your free time?**

> In my free time, I'm really into badminton. I play with my colleagues every Tuesday and Thursday after work. It's a good way to move after sitting at a computer all day. I also enjoy reading, mostly books about software design, but sometimes novels too. On weekends, I like taking my kids to the park. I'm not a big fan of shopping malls, because they're always crowded.

**Q5. How was your last weekend?**

> It was really relaxing. On Saturday morning, I slept in until eight, which is rare for me. Then I met two old friends from university at a coffee shop. We caught up for about three hours. In the evening, my wife and I cooked hotpot at home. On Sunday, we took the kids to the zoo. It was hot and a bit crowded, but the kids loved it. I went to bed early because I was exhausted.

**Q11. Do you think hobbies are important for software engineers?**

> Yes, I think so. Our job is mostly sitting and thinking, so a physical hobby like running or football helps us stay healthy. Also, when I'm stuck on a hard bug, I often find the answer while I'm doing something else. Hobbies give your brain a break.

## 7. Kịch bản cho buổi gia sư / AI

### Prompt dán vào AI (chế độ giọng nói)

```
You are my English conversation partner. I'm a Vietnamese software engineer
at about A2-B1 level. This week's topic is "hobbies and weekends".

Please:
1. Start by asking me how my last weekend was. Make me tell the story in the
   past tense, and ask for details (where, who, what happened, how I felt).
2. Then talk about hobbies. Share your own "hobbies" too, so it feels like a
   real conversation, and give me chances to ask YOU questions.
3. If I only give short answers, encourage me to say more.
4. Correct my past tense mistakes (went/goed, didn't went) briefly and continue.
5. At the end, tell me which "like/dislike" phrases I used and suggest 3 new
   ones I could use next time.
```

Bài tập thêm với AI (ngày T5): yêu cầu **bạn** là người hỏi: "This time, I will ask you about your hobbies. Answer naturally, and tell me if my questions sound natural."

### Với gia sư

- Kể cuối tuần vừa rồi trong 2 phút, nhờ gia sư ghi lại các lỗi quá khứ.
- Hỏi gia sư về sở thích của họ, cố gắng hỏi tiếp ít nhất 3 câu (*How did you get into it? What do you like most about it?*).
- Hỏi: "Is *I'm into...* too informal for a job interview?" (Trả lời thường gặp: dùng được khi nói chuyện thân thiện, phỏng vấn thì *I enjoy... / I'm interested in...* an toàn hơn.)
